# Argument

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

When calling a function, you pass actual values - arguments - that fill the parameter slots.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`add(3, 5)` - here 3 and 5 are the arguments. They fill parameters `a` and `b`.

## 🔗 Analogy

The ingredients you actually bring to the kitchen. The recipe (function) says 'bring flour and sugar' (parameters); you bring 200g flour and 100g sugar (arguments).
