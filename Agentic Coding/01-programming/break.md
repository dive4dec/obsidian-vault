# Break

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Sometimes you need to exit a loop early - you found what you were looking for, or something went wrong.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`for item in items: if item == target: print('Found!'); break` - stops the loop immediately.

## 🔗 Analogy

Finding your keys in a bag - you dig through items one by one, and the moment you find the keys, you stop searching.
