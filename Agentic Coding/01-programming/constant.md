# Constant

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Some values should never change - pi, the speed of light, a max score. Constants are variables you agree not to modify.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`MAX_LEVELS = 50` or `PI = 3.14159`. By convention, uppercase names mean `don`t change this'.

## 🔗 Analogy

A carved stone sign at a park entrance - 'Park Hours: 6am-10pm' is fixed and permanent. Everyone agrees not to change it.
