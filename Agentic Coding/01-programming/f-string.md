# F-String

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Building strings by concatenation is clunky. F-strings let you embed variables directly into text.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`name = 'Alice'; age = 14; print(f'My name is {name} and I am {age}')` - variables are inserted right into the string.

## 🔗 Analogy

A fill-in-the-blanks story - 'My name is ___ and I am ___' where the blanks are automatically filled with your variable values.
