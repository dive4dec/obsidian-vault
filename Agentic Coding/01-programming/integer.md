# Integer

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Many programs count things - steps, lives, items in inventory. Integers are whole numbers without decimals.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`lives = 3` or `temperature = -5`. You can do arithmetic: `3 * 4 = 12`.

## 🔗 Analogy

A counter on a scoreboard - it clicks up or down by whole clicks, never half a click.
