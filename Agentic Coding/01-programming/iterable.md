# Iterable

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Anything you can loop over with a `for` loop is iterable - lists, strings, dictionaries, files, and more.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`for x in [1,2,3]` - list is iterable. `for c in 'hi'` - string is iterable. `for k in {'a':1}` - dict is iterable (iterates over keys).

## 🔗 Analogy

A conveyor belt of items - as long as items keep coming, you can process them one at a time. The belt is the iterable.
