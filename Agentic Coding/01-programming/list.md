# List

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

You often need to store many related items together - a deck of cards, a class list, a shopping cart.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`fruits = ['apple', 'banana', 'cherry']`. Access by position: `fruits[0]` is `apple`. Add items: `fruits.append('date')`.

## 🔗 Analogy

A shopping list written on paper - items are in order, you can cross things off, add new ones, or look at any item by its position.
