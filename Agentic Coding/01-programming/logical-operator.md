# Logical Operator

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

You often need to combine conditions - age over 13 AND has permission, OR is an adult.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`age > 13 and has_permission` - both must be True. `is_admin or is_teacher` - either one being True is enough. `not is_banned`.

## 🔗 Analogy

Filtering at a theme park: you can ride if (tall enough) AND (not pregnant) OR (have a VIP pass). AND, OR, NOT combine the rules.
