# Statement

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A statement is a complete instruction that does something - it's a full sentence of code.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`x = 5` is a statement (assignment). `if x > 3: print(x)` is a statement (conditional). Statements don't produce values.

## 🔗 Analogy

A command from a teacher - 'Open your books' or 'Stand up'. It's a complete instruction, not a question with an answer.
