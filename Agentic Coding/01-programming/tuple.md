# Tuple

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Some data shouldn't change after creation - coordinates, dates, RGB colour values. Tuples are immutable lists.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`rgb = (255, 128, 0)` for orange. You can't change `rgb[0]` - it's locked. But it's faster and safer than a list.

## 🔗 Analogy

A sealed envelope - once you seal it, the contents can't be changed. You can read what's inside, but not swap it.
