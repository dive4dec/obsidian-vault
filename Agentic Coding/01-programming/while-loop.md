# While Loop

> **Domain:** [[_programming-fundamentals-moc|Programming Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

When you don't know how many times to repeat, but you know when to stop - keep going until a condition changes.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`while lives > 0: play_round()` - keeps playing as long as the player has lives left.

## 🔗 Analogy

Running laps around a track - you keep running while you still have energy. When energy hits zero, you stop.
