# Dequeue

> **Domain:** [[_data-structures-moc|Data Structures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Dequeue is the operation of removing and returning the front item from a queue.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`q.popleft()` removes and returns the item at the front. The next item moves up to become the new front.

## 🔗 Analogy

The person at the front of the queue gets their food and leaves. The second person steps up to the counter.
