# Directed Graph

> **Domain:** [[_data-structures-moc|Data Structures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

When relationships have a direction -- one-way streets, followers on social media, prerequisites -- edges point from one node to another.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Instagram follows: Alice follows Bob, but Bob does not follow Alice. The edge goes A -> B, not B -> A.

## 🔗 Analogy

One-way streets in a city. You can drive from street A to street B, but not back. The arrow shows the legal direction of travel.
