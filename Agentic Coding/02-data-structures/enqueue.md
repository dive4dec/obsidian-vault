# Enqueue

> **Domain:** [[_data-structures-moc|Data Structures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Enqueue is the operation of adding an item to the back of a queue.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`q.append('task3')` adds `task3` to the end of the queue. It will be processed after all earlier items.

## 🔗 Analogy

A new person joining the back of the lunch queue. They stand at the end and wait their turn.
