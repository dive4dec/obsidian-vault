# Max-Heap

> **Domain:** [[_data-structures-moc|Data Structures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A heap where the parent is always greater than or equal to its children. The maximum value sits at the root.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Array `[100, 50, 80, 30, 40]`: 100 at root, children 50 and 80, grandchildren 30 and 40. Every parent beats its children.

## 🔗 Analogy

A leaderboard where the highest scorer is always displayed at the top. When a new high score comes in, it bubbles up to the top.
