# Peek

> **Domain:** [[_data-structures-moc|Data Structures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Sometimes you need to see the top of a stack without removing it. Peek lets you look without modifying the stack.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`stack = [1, 2, 3]; top = stack[-1]` gives 3 without removing it. The stack stays `[1, 2, 3]`.

## 🔗 Analogy

Looking at the top plate through the glass door of the cupboard without actually opening the door and taking it.
