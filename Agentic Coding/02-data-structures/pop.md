# Pop

> **Domain:** [[_data-structures-moc|Data Structures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Pop is the operation of removing and returning the top item from a stack.

## 📋 Concrete Example

`stack = [1, 2, 3]; stack.pop()` returns 3 and the stack becomes `[1, 2]`. The top item is gone.

## 🔗 Analogy

Taking the top plate off the plate stack. You get that plate, and the one underneath becomes the new top.
