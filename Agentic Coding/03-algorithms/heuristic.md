# Heuristic

> **Domain:** [[_algorithms-moc|Algorithms]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A rule of thumb that guides search toward promising solutions. Not guaranteed optimal, but practical. The heart of A* and greedy algorithms.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In A* pathfinding, the heuristic 'straight-line distance to goal' guides the search. It is not the actual road distance, but it points in the right direction.

## 🔗 Analogy

A hiker estimating 'the summit is that way, uphill' -- not a precise route, but a useful guide. The hiker follows the heuristic and adjusts as they go.
