# Monte Carlo Method

> **Domain:** [[_algorithms-moc|Algorithms]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Use random sampling to approximate solutions for problems that are hard to compute exactly. More samples give better accuracy.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Estimate pi: throw darts at a square with an inscribed circle. The ratio of darts in the circle to total darts approaches pi/4. More darts = better estimate.

## 🔗 Analogy

Estimating how many students are in a building by randomly sampling rooms and extrapolating. More samples give a more accurate estimate without counting every student.
