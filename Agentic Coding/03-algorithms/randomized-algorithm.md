# Randomized Algorithm

> **Domain:** [[_algorithms-moc|Algorithms]]

## 🎯 Motivation

An algorithm that uses random numbers to make decisions. Often simpler and faster than deterministic alternatives, with high probability of correctness.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Randomised quick sort picks a random pivot. The worst case (O(n^2)) is possible but extremely unlikely. Average case is O(n log n) with high probability.

## 🔗 Analogy

Flipping a coin to choose which queue at the supermarket. You might not pick the fastest queue, but over many trips, your average wait time is good. Randomness avoids consistently bad choices.
