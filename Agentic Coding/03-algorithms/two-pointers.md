# Two Pointers

> **Domain:** [[_algorithms-moc|Algorithms]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Use two indices moving through the array to solve problems like pair-finding, reversal, or partitioning in O(n) with O(1) space.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Find two numbers that sum to 10 in sorted [1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9]: left=0 (1), right=5 (9). 1+9=10! Or: left=1 (3), right=4 (7). 3+7=10!

## 🔗 Analogy

Two people walking toward each other from opposite ends of a corridor, checking items as they meet. Much faster than one person checking every pair.
