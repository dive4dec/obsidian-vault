# Kubernetes

> **Domain:** [[_computer-systems-moc|Computer Systems]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Orchestration platform for containers. Manages deployment, scaling, and operations of containerized applications.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Kubernetes: deploy 10 containers of your web app. Kubernetes: starts them, monitors health, restarts crashed ones, scales to 20 during peak traffic, scales to 5 at night. Automated container management.

## 🔗 Analogy

Kubernetes is like a fleet manager for delivery trucks - they decide how many trucks (containers) are needed, route them efficiently, replace broken ones, and add more during busy periods. Automated fleet management for containers.
