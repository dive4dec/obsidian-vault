# Orchestration

> **Domain:** [[_computer-systems-moc|Computer Systems]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Automated management of multiple services: deployment, scaling, failover, load balancing.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Orchestration: 50 microservices running. Orchestrator: deploys updates one at a time (no downtime), scales busy services, restarts failed ones, balances traffic. All automated.

## 🔗 Analogy

Orchestration is like a symphony conductor - they coordinate all musicians (services), ensure timing, adjust volume, and handle mistakes. Orchestration coordinates multiple services for harmonious operation.
