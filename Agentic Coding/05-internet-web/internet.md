# Internet

> **Domain:** [[_internet-web-moc|Internet & Web]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The internet connects billions of devices worldwide. Understanding it is essential because AI agents live on the internet - they fetch data, call APIs, and serve users globally.

## 📋 Concrete Example

You type google.com. Your request travels through undersea cables, reaches Google's server, and returns results in 50ms. The internet makes this possible for billions of people simultaneously.

## 🔗 Analogy

The internet is like a global postal system - your letter (data) goes from your house to a post office (router) to another post office to the destination. Billions of letters travel simultaneously, each reaching the right address.
