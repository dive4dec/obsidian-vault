# SSL Certificate

> **Domain:** [[_internet-web-moc|Internet & Web]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A digital certificate that proves a website's identity and enables HTTPS encryption.

## 📋 Concrete Example

SSL certificate: issued by a Certificate Authority. Contains: domain name, owner, expiration. Browser checks: is this certificate valid? Yes -> shows the lock icon. No -> warns the user.

## 🔗 Analogy

An SSL certificate is like a passport - it proves identity (this is really google.com) and is issued by a trusted authority (Certificate Authority). The browser checks it, just like immigration checks your passport.
