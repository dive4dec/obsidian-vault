# Push

> **Domain:** [[_software-engineering-moc|Software Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Uploading your local commits to a remote repository. Shares your changes with the team.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Push: 'git push origin main'. Your local commits are uploaded to GitHub. Teammates can now pull them. Your changes are shared.

## 🔗 Analogy

Pushing is like submitting homework - you did the work locally (commit), now you submit it to the teacher (remote repo). The teacher and classmates can see your work.
