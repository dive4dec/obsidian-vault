# Sprint Planning

> **Domain:** [[_software-engineering-moc|Software Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Choosing which tasks to work on in the next sprint. Based on priorities and capacity.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Planning: 10 tasks in the backlog. Team capacity: 6 tasks in 2 weeks. Choose the top 6 by priority. Assign owners. Sprint starts. Focused, realistic plan.

## 🔗 Analogy

Sprint planning is like planning a road trip - you have 10 destinations (tasks) but only 5 days (capacity). You pick the 5 best destinations and plan the route. Realistic, prioritized.
