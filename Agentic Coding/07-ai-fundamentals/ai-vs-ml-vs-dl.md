# AI vs ML vs DL

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Computers can do smart things in different ways. Knowing the difference helps you pick the right tool.

## 📋 Concrete Example

AI is the big umbrella (any program that seems smart). ML is a subset where the program learns from data. DL is a subset of ML using neural networks with many layers.

## 🔗 Analogy

AI is like cooking food in general. ML is cooking by tasting and adjusting. DL is cooking with a fancy multi-layer oven that learns recipes itself.
