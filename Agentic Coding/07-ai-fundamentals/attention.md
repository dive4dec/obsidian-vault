# Attention

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Lets the model focus on the most relevant parts of the input when making each prediction.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Translating the bank of the river, attention links bank to river not to money-bank.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a student skimming a long article and highlighting only the sentences relevant to the question.
