# Biases

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A bias shifts the neuron output so it can fire even when inputs are zero, giving the model flexibility.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A neuron with bias = -2 will not fire easily. Changing bias to +3 makes it fire more often for the same inputs.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a thermostat offset - even if the room temperature reading is the same, you can nudge the system to heat earlier.
