# Clustering

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Grouping similar items together without labels. A key unsupervised task.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Group shoppers by buying habits into clusters like weekly groceries, luxury, impulse buyers.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like organizing a big box of mixed buttons into piles by color and size, with no labels given.
