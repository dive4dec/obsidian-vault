# Dropout

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Randomly turns off some neurons during training so the network does not rely too much on any one path.

## 📋 Concrete Example

During each training step, 20% of neurons are randomly dropped. At inference, all neurons are used.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a sports coach benching random players during practice so the team learns to work together, not depend on one star.
