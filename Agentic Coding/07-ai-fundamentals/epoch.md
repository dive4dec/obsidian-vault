# Epoch

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

One full pass through the entire training dataset. Training runs for many epochs.

## 📋 Concrete Example

With 1000 images and batch size 100, one epoch = 10 batches = 10 weight updates.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like reading a textbook cover to cover once. You read it many times to really learn it.
