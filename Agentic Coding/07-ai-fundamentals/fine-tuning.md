# Fine-Tuning

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Take a model already trained on a big general dataset and train it a bit more on your specific data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Start with a model trained on general English text, then fine-tune on Hong Kong legal documents.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a general mechanic taking a short course to specialize in electric cars - building on existing skills.
