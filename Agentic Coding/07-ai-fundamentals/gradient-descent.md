# Gradient Descent

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Adjusts weights step by step downhill on the loss surface to reduce error.

## 📋 Concrete Example

If the loss goes down when you increase weight A, gradient descent increases weight A a little. Repeat.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like hiking down a foggy mountain by always stepping in the steepest downward direction.
