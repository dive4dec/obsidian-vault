# Loss Function

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures how wrong the predictions are. Training tries to make this number as small as possible.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Mean squared error: average of (prediction - true value) squared across examples.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a scoreboard that shows your mistakes - the lower the score, the better you are doing.
