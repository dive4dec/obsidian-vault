# Neuron

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The basic unit of a neural network. It combines inputs and decides how much signal to pass forward.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A neuron takes 3 numbers, multiplies each by a weight, adds a bias, then applies ReLU. Output goes to the next layer.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a single voter who weighs different rumors (inputs), adds their own bias, and decides whether to shout (activate).
