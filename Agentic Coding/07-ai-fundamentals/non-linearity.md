# Non-Linearity

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The ability to model curved, complex relationships, not just straight lines. Comes from activation functions.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Without non-linearity, a 100-layer network collapses to the equivalent of one linear layer.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like being able to draw curves and squiggles, not just rulers-straight lines, on a graph.
