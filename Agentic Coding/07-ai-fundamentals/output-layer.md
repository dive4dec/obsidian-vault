# Output Layer

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The final layer that produces the prediction or decision.

## 📋 Concrete Example

For 10-digit classification, the output layer has 10 neurons, one per digit, with softmax probabilities.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the cash register - it gives you the final answer after all the behind-the-scenes work.
