# Parameters

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The numbers inside the model (weights and biases) that get learned from data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GPT-3 has about 175 billion parameters. Each is a number adjusted during training.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the knobs and dials on an old radio - millions of them, tuned until the signal comes in clear.
