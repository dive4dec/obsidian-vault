# Perceptron

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The simplest neural unit - a single neuron that makes a yes/no decision. The ancestor of modern networks.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A perceptron takes exam score and homework score, multiplies by weights, and outputs pass or fail.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a strict teacher who adds up weighted scores and says pass or fail based on a cutoff.
