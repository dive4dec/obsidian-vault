# Pre-Training

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Training a model on a huge general dataset before fine-tuning on a specific task.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Pre-train a language model on all of Wikipedia, then fine-tune it for answering science questions.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a student doing general reading for years before specializing in a university major.
