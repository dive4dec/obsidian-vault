# Regression

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Predicting a continuous number instead of a category.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Predict the price of a house (a number like 4.5 million) from its features.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a weather forecast predicting tomorrow temperature will be 27.3 degrees - a number, not a category.
