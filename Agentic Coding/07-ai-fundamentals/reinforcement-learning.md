# Reinforcement Learning

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

An agent learns by trying actions and getting rewards or penalties, improving over time.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A robot learns to walk: each step forward gives reward, falling gives penalty. Over many tries it learns a good walking policy.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like training a dog with treats and scolding - it figures out good behavior by trial and error.
