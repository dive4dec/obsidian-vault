# ReLU

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A simple, fast activation that keeps positives and zeroes negatives. Used in most deep networks.

## 📋 Concrete Example

ReLU(5) = 5, ReLU(-3) = 0. It is just max(0, x).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a bouncer who lets in guests with positive vibes and turns away negative ones, no questions asked.
