# Softmax

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Turns a list of numbers into probabilities that sum to 1. Used in classification output layers.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Raw scores [2.0, 1.0, 0.1] become probabilities [0.7, 0.2, 0.1] after softmax.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a teacher converting raw test scores into percentages that add up to 100%.
