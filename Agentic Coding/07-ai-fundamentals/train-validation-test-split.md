# Train Validation Test Split

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Divide data into three parts: train to learn, validation to tune, test to final-check.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Split 10000 examples: 8000 train, 1000 validation, 1000 test. Only touch test once at the end.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like splitting your study time: past papers (train), mock exam (validation), real exam (test).
