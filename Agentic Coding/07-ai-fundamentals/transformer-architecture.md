# Transformer Architecture

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A neural network design based on attention that powers modern language models like GPT.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A transformer reads a whole sentence at once and uses attention to figure out which words matter for each other.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a team meeting where everyone listens to everyone and each person decides who to pay attention to.
