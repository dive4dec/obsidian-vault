# Underfitting

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The model is too simple to capture the pattern, doing poorly even on training data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Trying to fit a straight line to data that is clearly curved - it misses the shape entirely.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like using a blunt knife to carve a fine sculpture - you just cannot capture the detail.
