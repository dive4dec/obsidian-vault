# Unsupervised Learning

> **Domain:** [[_ai-fundamentals-moc|AI Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Sometimes data has no labels. We still want to find hidden structure or groups.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Give a shop customer purchase data with no labels. The algorithm groups customers into clusters (e.g. bargain hunters vs big spenders).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like sorting a big pile of mixed Lego bricks into piles by shape and color without anyone telling you the categories first.
