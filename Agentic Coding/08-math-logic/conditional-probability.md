# Conditional Probability

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The probability of an event given that another event has already happened.

## 📋 Concrete Example

P(rain | dark clouds) is much higher than P(rain) in general. Knowing clouds change the odds.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the probability your bus is late changes once you hear there was an accident on the road.
