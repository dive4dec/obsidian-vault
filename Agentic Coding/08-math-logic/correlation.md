# Correlation

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures how two variables move together. Correlation is not causation.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Ice cream sales and drowning deaths both rise in summer - correlated, but ice cream does not cause drowning.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like two dancers moving together - they might be following the same music, not one leading the other.
