# Cosine Similarity

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures the angle between two vectors, ignoring their length. Great for comparing meaning.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Vectors for king and queen have a small angle (high cosine similarity, about 0.7). King and banana have a large angle.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like comparing the direction two people are walking - same direction means similar, regardless of speed.
