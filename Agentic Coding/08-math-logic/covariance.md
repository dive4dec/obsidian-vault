# Covariance

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures how two variables change together. Positive means they rise together; negative means one rises as the other falls.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Study hours and grades have positive covariance. TV hours and grades might have negative covariance.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like two friends on a seesaw - if one goes up while the other goes down, that is negative covariance.
