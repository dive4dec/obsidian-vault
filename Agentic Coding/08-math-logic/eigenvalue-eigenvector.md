# Eigenvalue Eigenvector

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Special vectors that only stretch or shrink under a transformation (eigenvectors), by a factor (eigenvalues).

## 📋 Concrete Example

PCA finds eigenvectors of the covariance matrix. The largest eigenvalues mark the most important directions.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like finding the natural grain of a piece of wood - along the grain (eigenvector) it stretches evenly, by the eigenvalue amount.
