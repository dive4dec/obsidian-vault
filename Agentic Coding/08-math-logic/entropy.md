# Entropy

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures uncertainty or randomness. Higher entropy means more chaos, less predictability.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A fair coin has entropy of 1 bit. A loaded coin with 99% heads has much lower entropy.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the messiness of a bedroom - a tidy room (predictable) has low entropy, a chaotic one has high.
