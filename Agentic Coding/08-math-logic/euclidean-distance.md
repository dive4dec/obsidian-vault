# Euclidean Distance

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The straight-line distance between two points. Used to measure similarity in feature space.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Distance between [0,0] and [3,4] = square root of (9+16) = 5.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like measuring with a ruler between two dots on a map - the direct straight line.
