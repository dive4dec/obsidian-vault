# Exponent

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Repeated multiplication. The inverse of logarithm. Growth can be explosive.

## 📋 Concrete Example

2 to the power 10 = 1024. Exponential growth doubles each step: 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, ...

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a rumor spreading where each person tells 2 new people - the numbers explode quickly.
