# Exponential Growth

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Growth that multiplies by a constant each step, not adds. How things go viral.

## 📋 Concrete Example

If followers double each day: 1, 2, 4, 8, 16... After 20 days, over 1 million.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a chain letter - each person who gets it sends it to more, and the numbers explode.
