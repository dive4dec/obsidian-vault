# Gradient

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A vector pointing in the direction of steepest increase. Negative gradient points toward decrease.

## 📋 Concrete Example

On a hill, the gradient points uphill. To descend, you go in the opposite direction.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a compass that always points to the steepest way up a mountain; flip it to find the way down.
