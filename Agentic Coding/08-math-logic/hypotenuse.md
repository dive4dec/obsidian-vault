# Hypotenuse

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The longest side of a right triangle, opposite the right angle. Foundation of distance calculations.

## 📋 Concrete Example

For sides 3 and 4, the hypotenuse is 5 (Pythagorean theorem: 3 squared + 4 squared = 5 squared).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the shortcut across a rectangular field - longer than either side, but the direct route.
