# Information Theory

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The study of quantifying information, uncertainty, and communication. Foundation of modern ML loss functions.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Shannon entropy measures the average bits needed to encode messages from a source.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a postal pricing system - it costs more bits to send surprising news than predictable news.
