# KL Divergence

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures how one probability distribution differs from another. Not symmetric - direction matters.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Compare a predicted word distribution to the true one. KL divergence quantifies the gap.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like measuring how different your map is from the real city layout - useful for knowing how much to correct.
