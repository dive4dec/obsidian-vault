# Local Minimum

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A low point that is not the lowest overall. Gradient descent can get stuck here.

## 📋 Concrete Example

On a bumpy loss surface, the gradient might lead to a small valley that is not the deepest point.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like resting in a small ditch on a mountainside - you are low locally, but not at the valley floor.
