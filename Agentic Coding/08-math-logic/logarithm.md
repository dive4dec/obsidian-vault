# Logarithm

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The inverse of exponentiation. Compresses huge ranges into manageable numbers. Used in loss functions and probabilities.

## 📋 Concrete Example

log base 10 of 1000 = 3, because 10 to the power 3 = 1000. log turns multiplication into addition.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a zoom lens that shrinks vast distances so you can see them on one map.
