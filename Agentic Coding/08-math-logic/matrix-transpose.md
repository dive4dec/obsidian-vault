# Matrix Transpose

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Flips a matrix over its diagonal, swapping rows and columns. Used everywhere in ML computations.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Transpose of [[1,2],[3,4],[5,6]] (3x2) becomes [[1,3,5],[2,4,6]] (2x3).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like turning a photo sideways - rows become columns and columns become rows.
