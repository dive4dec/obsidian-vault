# Maximum Likelihood

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Choose parameters that make the observed data most probable. The principle behind many ML training procedures.

## 📋 Concrete Example

If data looks bell-shaped, pick the mean and variance that make that bell curve fit best.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a detective picking the story that best explains all the evidence - the most likely scenario.
