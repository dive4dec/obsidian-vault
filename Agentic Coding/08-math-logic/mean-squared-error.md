# Mean Squared Error

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The average of squared errors. Penalizes large errors more than small ones.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Errors of 2 and 4: squared to 4 and 16, averaged to 10. The 4-error dominates.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a test that punishes big mistakes extra hard - a 10-point miss hurts more than five 2-point misses.
