# Mean

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The average of a set of numbers. The simplest summary of the center of data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Scores [70, 80, 90]. Mean = (70+80+90)/3 = 80.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like sharing all the class candy equally - everyone gets the average amount.
