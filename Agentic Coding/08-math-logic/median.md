# Median

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The middle value when data is sorted. Not affected by extreme outliers.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Salaries sorted: [10k, 20k, 25k, 30k, 1M]. Median = 25k. Mean would be dragged up by the 1M.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the middle person in a line sorted by height - one giant at the end does not change them.
