# Normal Distribution

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The bell curve. Most data clusters around the mean, with fewer values at the extremes.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Heights of students: most near the average, few very short or very tall. Classic bell shape.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a sand pile - most sand heaps in the middle, with thinner slopes on the sides.
