# Probability

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Measures how likely an event is, from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). The language of uncertainty in AI.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The probability of rolling a 6 on a fair die is 1/6, about 0.167.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a weather forecast saying 70% chance of rain - a number between 0 and 1 for how likely.
