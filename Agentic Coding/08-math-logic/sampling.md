# Sampling

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Picking items from a distribution. Used to generate text, augment data, and estimate statistics.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A language model samples the next word from its probability distribution, adding variety to outputs.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like drawing names from a hat - you pick one based on the probabilities, not always the most likely.
