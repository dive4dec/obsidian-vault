# Sigmoid Derivative

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The slope of sigmoid, used in backpropagation. Maxes at 0.25, which causes vanishing gradients in deep networks.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Sigmoid derivative at x=0 is 0.25 (steepest). At extremes it approaches 0, meaning gradients vanish.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a hill that is flattest at the top and bottom - hard to slide down when there is no slope.
