# Softmax

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Converts a vector of scores into probabilities that sum to 1. The standard output for multi-class classification.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Scores [1, 2, 3] become probabilities [0.09, 0.24, 0.67] after softmax. The biggest score gets the most probability.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like an election that converts vote counts into percentages - all percentages add up to 100%.
