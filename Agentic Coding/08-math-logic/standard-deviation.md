# Standard Deviation

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The square root of variance, in the same units as the data. Easier to interpret.

## 📋 Concrete Example

If variance is 25, standard deviation is 5. Most data falls within 2 standard deviations of the mean.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a margin of error - plus or minus 5 marks tells you how spread out exam scores typically are.
