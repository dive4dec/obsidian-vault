# Statistics

> **Domain:** [[_math-logic-for-ai-moc|Math & Logic for AI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The science of collecting, summarizing, and interpreting data. Essential for understanding ML results.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Computing mean, median, and standard deviation of student test scores to understand class performance.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a sports commentator summarizing a match with stats - possession, shots, passes - to tell the story.
