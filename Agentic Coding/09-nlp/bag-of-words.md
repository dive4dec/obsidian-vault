# Bag of Words

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Represents text as a count of each word, ignoring order. Simple but loses meaning and structure.

## 📋 Concrete Example

I love AI and AI loves me becomes {I:1, love:1, AI:2, and:1, loves:1, me:1}. Order is gone.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like tossing all words from a sentence into a bag - you know what words are there but not the order.
