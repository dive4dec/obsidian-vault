# BERT

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers. Reads text in both directions for deep understanding.

## 📋 Concrete Example

BERT is trained with masked language modeling. It is great for classification and question answering, not generation.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a reader who can look at the whole page at once, both left and right, to understand each word.
