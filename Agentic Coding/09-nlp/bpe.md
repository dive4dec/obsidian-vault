# BPE

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Byte-Pair Encoding starts with characters and merges the most frequent pairs step by step. Used by GPT models.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Start with characters. low + est merge to lowest if seen often. Build a vocabulary of frequent subwords.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a factory that starts with tiny parts and keeps gluing the most common pairs together until it has useful modules.
