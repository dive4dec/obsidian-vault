# Chunking

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Grouping tokens into meaningful phrases (noun phrases, verb phrases). A step between tokens and full parsing.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The big red car becomes [The big red car] as one noun phrase chunk.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like grouping words into Lego blocks - each block is a phrase you can move around.
