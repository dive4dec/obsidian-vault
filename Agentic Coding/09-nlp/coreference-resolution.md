# Coreference Resolution

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Finding which words refer to the same entity. Essential for understanding pronouns.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In Mary said she was tired, coreference resolution links she to Mary.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a detective connecting nicknames, pronouns, and names to figure out who is who in a story.
