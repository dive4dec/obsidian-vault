# Corpus

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A large collection of text used for training. The bigger and more diverse, the better the model learns.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Wikipedia, news articles, and books together form a corpus for training a language model.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a library the model studies - the more books it reads, the more it knows.
