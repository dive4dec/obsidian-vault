# Decoder

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The part of a transformer that generates the output sequence one token at a time, using encoder context.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In translation, the decoder generates the English translation word by word, guided by the encoder output.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a writer who takes the understanding and writes it out in a new language, one word at a time.
