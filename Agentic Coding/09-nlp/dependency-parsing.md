# Dependency Parsing

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Analyzes the grammatical structure by finding relationships (head and dependent) between words.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In I eat rice, eat is the head, I is the subject dependent, and rice is the object dependent.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like drawing arrows between words to show who depends on whom in a sentence.
