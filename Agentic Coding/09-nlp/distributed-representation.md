# Distributed Representation

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Representing meaning as a pattern across many dimensions, not a single symbol. Embeddings do this.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The meaning of king is spread across 300 numbers, not stored in one slot labeled king.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like describing a color by its RGB values instead of just its name - more detailed and comparable.
