# Embedding

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Turns words or tokens into vectors of numbers so the computer can measure meaning and similarity.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The word happy might become [0.8, -0.1, 0.5, ...]. Words with similar meaning get similar vectors.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like giving each word a fingerprint - similar words have similar prints, different words have different ones.
