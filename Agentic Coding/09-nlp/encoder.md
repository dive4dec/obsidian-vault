# Encoder

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The part of a transformer that reads and understands the input, turning it into rich representations.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In translation, the encoder reads the Chinese sentence and produces a set of context vectors capturing its meaning.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a reader who absorbs a story and forms a deep understanding of it in their mind.
