# F1 Score

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The harmonic mean of precision and recall. Balances both into one number for classification tasks.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Precision 0.8, recall 0.6 -> F1 = 2 * (0.8*0.6)/(0.8+0.6) = 0.68. A single balanced score.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a judge who weighs both how many correct answers you got (precision) and how many you missed (recall).
