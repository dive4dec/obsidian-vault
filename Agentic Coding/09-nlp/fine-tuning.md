# Fine-Tuning

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Training a pre-trained language model further on a specific dataset so it specializes in a task or domain.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Fine-tune GPT on Hong Kong legal documents to make a legal assistant chatbot.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a general doctor taking extra training to become a heart specialist.
