# GloVe

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Global Vectors: learns embeddings from word co-occurrence counts across the whole corpus.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GloVe looks at how often ice and steam appear near each other versus with other words, and places them accordingly.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a popularity chart - words that hang out together in many books get similar scores.
