# GPT

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Generative Pre-trained Transformer. Autoregressive decoder that generates text token by token.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GPT reads your prompt and generates a response one token at a time, always looking left only.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a storyteller who builds the tale word by word, each new word based on everything said so far.
