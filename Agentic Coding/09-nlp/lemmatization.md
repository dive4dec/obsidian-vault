# Lemmatization

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Reduces words to their dictionary root form (lemma). Slower than stemming but more accurate.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Better becomes good. Running becomes run. Studies becomes study. All are real dictionary words.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like looking up a word in a dictionary to find its base form - accurate but takes a moment.
