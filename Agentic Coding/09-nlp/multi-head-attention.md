# Multi-Head Attention

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Runs several attention mechanisms in parallel, each focusing on different relationships, then combines results.

## 📋 Concrete Example

One head might focus on grammar, another on emotion, another on named entities - all at once.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a team of detectives each looking at different clues (motive, alibi, evidence) and then pooling findings.
