# Named Entity Recognition

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Finds and classifies names in text: people, places, organizations, dates, etc.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In Tim Cook visited Apple Park in Cupertino, NER finds: Tim Cook (PERSON), Apple Park (ORG), Cupertino (LOC).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a highlighter that marks every proper noun and labels what kind of thing it is.
