# Part of Speech Tagging

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Labels each word with its grammatical role: noun, verb, adjective, etc. Helps disambiguate meaning.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In I book a ticket, book is a verb. In I read a book, book is a noun. Same word, different tags.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like labeling each word in a sentence with a name tag saying I am a noun or I am a verb.
