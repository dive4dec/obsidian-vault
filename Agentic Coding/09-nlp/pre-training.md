# Pre-Training

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Training a language model on a massive text corpus before any specific task. Gives general language understanding.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GPT is pre-trained on web text, books, and code. Then it is fine-tuned or prompted for specific tasks.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a student doing broad reading for years before specializing in one subject at university.
