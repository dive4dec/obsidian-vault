# Precision

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Of the items the model predicted as positive, how many were actually positive. Measures quality of positive predictions.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Model predicts 10 emails are spam, 8 actually are. Precision = 8/10 = 0.8. 2 were false alarms.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a fisherman checking his catch - of the 10 fish he kept, how many were the target species.
