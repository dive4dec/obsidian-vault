# Preprocessing

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Cleaning and preparing raw text before feeding it to a model. Tokenization, lowercasing, removing noise.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Raw HTML gets tags stripped, text lowercased, tokenized, and stop words removed before analysis.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like washing and chopping vegetables before cooking - you prepare the ingredients first.
