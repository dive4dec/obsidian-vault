# Self-Attention

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Each token looks at all other tokens in the same sequence to understand its own meaning in context.

## 📋 Concrete Example

In The bark was loud, self-attention helps the model know bark means dog sound (not tree bark) by looking at loud.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a group discussion where each person listens to everyone else to decide what they themselves mean.
