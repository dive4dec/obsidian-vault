# Semantics

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The meaning of words and sentences. NLP aims to capture semantics, not just syntax.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The cat sat on the mat and The feline rested on the rug have different syntax but similar semantics.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like the difference between what a sentence says (syntax) and what it means (semantics).
