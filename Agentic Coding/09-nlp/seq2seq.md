# Seq2Seq

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Sequence-to-sequence models take an input sequence and produce an output sequence of different length.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Translation: input 5 Chinese words, output 7 English words. The lengths differ, and that is fine.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a translator who reads a 3-line Chinese poem and writes a 4-line English version.
