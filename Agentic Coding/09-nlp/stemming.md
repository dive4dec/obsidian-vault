# Stemming

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Cuts off word endings to get a root form. Fast but crude - results may not be real words.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Running, runs, runner all stem to run. Studies might stem to studi (not a real word).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like pruning a tree with hedge clippers - quick and rough, not always pretty.
