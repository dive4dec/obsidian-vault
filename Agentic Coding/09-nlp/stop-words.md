# Stop Words

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Common words like the, is, a that appear everywhere but carry little meaning. Often removed to save space.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Removing stop words from The cat sat on the mat gives cat sat mat. The core meaning stays.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like skimming a book and skipping filler words like um and uh to get to the real content.
