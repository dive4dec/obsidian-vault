# Subword Tokenization

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Splits text into pieces smaller than words but bigger than characters. Handles rare and new words well.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Unbelievable might split into [Un, believe, able]. ChatGPT splits text into subword units like this.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like breaking a big Lego build into medium modules - flexible enough to build anything, efficient to store.
