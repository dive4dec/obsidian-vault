# Summarization

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Producing a shorter version of a longer text while keeping the key points.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Input: 3-page news article. Output: 2-sentence summary capturing the headline event.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like writing a TL;DR at the top of a long email - the essence in a fraction of the words.
