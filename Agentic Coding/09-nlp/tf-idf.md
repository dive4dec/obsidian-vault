# TF-IDF

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Term Frequency x Inverse Document Frequency. Highlights words that are important in a document but rare overall.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The appears in every document (low IDF, ignored). Quantum might appear in one physics paper (high TF-IDF, important).

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a word's VIP score - frequent in this document but rare elsewhere means it is a keyword.
