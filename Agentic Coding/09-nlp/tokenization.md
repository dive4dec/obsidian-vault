# Tokenization

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Computers cannot read whole sentences at once. We must split text into smaller pieces called tokens first.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The sentence I love AI splits into tokens: [I, love, AI]. Each token gets a number from the vocabulary.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like cutting a long sandwich into bite-size pieces before serving - easier to eat and count.
