# Transformer Architecture

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The neural network design based entirely on attention, no recurrence. Powers all modern LLMs.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A transformer uses self-attention layers and feed-forward layers, processing all tokens at once in parallel.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a round-table meeting where everyone speaks and listens at once, instead of a phone chain where messages pass one by one.
