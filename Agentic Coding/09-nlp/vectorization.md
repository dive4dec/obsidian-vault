# Vectorization

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Converting text into numerical vectors that a model can process. Bag of words, TF-IDF, and embeddings all do this.

## 📋 Concrete Example

I love AI becomes [0.2, 0.8, 0.1, ...] via embedding. Now the computer can do math on it.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like translating a poem into numbers - each word and its meaning become coordinates on a map.
