# Word Tokenization

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Splitting text by spaces and punctuation into individual words. The simplest tokenization method.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Hello, world! becomes [Hello, ,, world, !]. Punctuation marks are often separate tokens.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like tearing a sentence into individual word cards, one word per card.
