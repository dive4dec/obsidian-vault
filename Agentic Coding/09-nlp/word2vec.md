# Word2Vec

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Learns word embeddings by predicting words from context. Similar words end up with similar vectors.

## 📋 Concrete Example

After training, vector(king) - vector(man) + vector(woman) is close to vector(queen). Famous word analogy.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like a GPS for meaning - words that mean similar things end up close together on the map.
