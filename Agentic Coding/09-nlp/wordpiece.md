# WordPiece

> **Domain:** [[_nlp-fundamentals-moc|NLP Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Like BPE but builds vocabulary for language understanding (BERT). Uses ## prefix for non-starting pieces.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Playing splits to [Play, `##ing`]. The double-hash marks that `ing` attaches to the previous piece.

## 🔗 Analogy

Like labeling puzzle pieces - the piece with ## is a connector that must follow another piece.
