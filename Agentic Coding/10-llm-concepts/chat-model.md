# Chat Model

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A model fine-tuned specifically for conversation (multi-turn chat) rather than single text completion. Most modern LLMs are chat models.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Base model: completes text ('The capital of France is' -> 'Paris, which is known for...'). Chat model: responds to messages (User: 'What is the capital of France?' -> `Paris`).

## 🔗 Analogy

A debate club member vs a public speaker - the public speaker gives monologues (base model). The debater responds to opponents in a back-and-forth (chat model). Chat models are trained for dialogue.
