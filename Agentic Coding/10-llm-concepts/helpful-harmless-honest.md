# Helpful Harmless Honest

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The three HHH goals of alignment: the model should be helpful (solve the user's problem), harmless (not cause damage), and honest (not deceive).

## 📋 Concrete Example

Helpful: gives a useful answer. Harmless: refuses to help with weapons. Honest: admits uncertainty instead of making things up. Sometimes these conflict - harmless may require refusing a `helpful` request.

## 🔗 Analogy

A good doctor - helpful (treats your illness), harmless (won't prescribe dangerous drugs), honest (tells you the truth about your condition). All three are essential; balancing them when they conflict is the challenge.
