# Large Language Model

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

To understand and generate human-like text at scale, we need a model trained on massive amounts of text data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GPT-4, Claude, and GLM are LLMs. You type a question, they generate an answer based on patterns learned from billions of text passages.

## 🔗 Analogy

A student who has read every book in the library - when you ask them a question, they combine knowledge from many books to give you an answer. They don't memorize; they learn patterns.
