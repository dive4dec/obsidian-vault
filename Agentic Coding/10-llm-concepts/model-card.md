# Model Card

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A documentation page describing what a model can do, its limitations, intended use, and performance metrics. Helps users choose the right model.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GPT-4's model card: 'Excels at reasoning and coding. May hallucinate facts. Not suitable for medical diagnosis.' Reading it helps you know when to use it and when not to.

## 🔗 Analogy

A nutrition label on food packaging - it tells you what's inside, allergens, calories, and serving size. You read it to decide if this product is right for you. Model cards do this for AI models.
