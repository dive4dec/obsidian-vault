# Multi-Head Attention

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Words relate to each other in multiple ways simultaneously - grammar, meaning, emotion. Multiple attention `heads` capture different relationships at once.

## 📋 Concrete Example

One head might track grammar (subject-verb), another tracks emotion (positive/negative words), another tracks entities (person names). Together they give a richer understanding.

## 🔗 Analogy

A group of detectives working the same case - one examines fingerprints, another interviews witnesses, another analyzes finances. Each focuses on different evidence; together they solve the case.
