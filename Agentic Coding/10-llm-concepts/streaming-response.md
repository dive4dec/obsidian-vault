# Streaming Response

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Instead of waiting for the entire response to be generated, streaming sends tokens as they're produced, so the user sees text appearing in real-time.

## 📋 Concrete Example

When ChatGPT types out its answer word by word - that's streaming. Without it, you'd stare at a blank screen for 10 seconds, then see the whole answer at once.

## 🔗 Analogy

A live sports broadcast vs a recording - live (streaming) shows the action as it happens. A recording shows everything at once after it's done. Streaming feels faster and more engaging.
