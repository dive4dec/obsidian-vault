# Temperature

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Temperature controls randomness in LLM output. Low temperature = focused and predictable. High temperature = creative and varied.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Temperature 0: 'What color is grass?' always gives `Green.` Temperature 1: might give 'Green, usually, though some varieties can be yellowish or brown in drought.' Temperature 2: might give nonsense.

## 🔗 Analogy

A chef's creativity dial - at 0, they follow the recipe exactly every time. At 0.5, they might add a twist. At 1.0, they improvise freely. At 2.0, they might throw in random ingredients (chaos).
