# Zero-Shot Learning

> **Domain:** [[_llm-concepts-moc|LLM Concepts]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Some LLMs are powerful enough to perform tasks without any examples - just the instruction. This is zero-shot learning.

## 📋 Concrete Example

'Classify the sentiment of this review: The food was amazing!' - No examples needed. The model just does it. Large models can do this for many tasks.

## 🔗 Analogy

A skilled worker who can figure out a task from instructions alone - 'sort these by size' needs no examples for an experienced worker. They just do it. Zero-shot is this ability in LLMs.
