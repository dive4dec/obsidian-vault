# Clarity

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Clear, unambiguous prompts prevent the model from guessing what you mean. Clarity is the #1 rule of prompt engineering.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Unclear: 'Make it better.' Clear: 'Fix the grammar errors in this paragraph. Do not change the meaning. Keep the same tone.' The model knows exactly what to do.

## 🔗 Analogy

Giving directions to a stranger - 'go that way' (unclear) vs 'walk straight for 100 meters, turn left at the 7-Eleven, it is the blue building on your right' (clear). Clarity prevents getting lost.
