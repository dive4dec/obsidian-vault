# Meta-Prompting

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Asking the model to help create or improve prompts. The model becomes a prompt engineering assistant.

## 📋 Concrete Example

'Here is my prompt: [prompt]. Suggest 3 improvements to make it more effective.' The model analyzes and suggests: 'Add specificity about output format, include an example, add constraints.'

## 🔗 Analogy

A writing tutor reviewing your essay - instead of writing the essay for you, they suggest improvements. Meta-prompting has the LLM review and improve your prompts rather than just answering them.
