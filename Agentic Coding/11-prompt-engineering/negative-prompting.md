# Negative Prompting

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Telling the model what NOT to do is sometimes as important as telling it what to do. Negative prompts prevent unwanted behavior.

## 📋 Concrete Example

'Explain quantum physics. Do NOT use jargon. Do NOT mention Schrodinger. Do NOT exceed 100 words.' The constraints prevent common pitfalls.

## 🔗 Analogy

Instructions to a photographer - 'Take a portrait. Do NOT shoot from below. Do NOT use flash. Do NOT center the subject.' Knowing what to avoid is as important as knowing what to do.
