# Persona

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A persona is a detailed character profile you give the LLM - not just a role, but personality, expertise level, and communication style.

## 📋 Concrete Example

'You are Dr. Lee, a patient and encouraging math tutor who loves using food analogies. You never give direct answers - you guide students step by step.' This persona produces consistently styled responses.

## 🔗 Analogy

Creating a character for a role-play game - you define their background, personality, and skills. When you play as that character, you stay in character. A persona keeps the LLM's responses consistent.
