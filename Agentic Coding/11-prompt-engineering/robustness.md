# Robustness

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A robust prompt works well across a wide range of inputs, including unexpected and edge cases, without failing or producing nonsense.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A robust translation prompt handles: short phrases, long documents, idioms ('it is raining cats and dogs'), slang, technical jargon, and mixed languages. A fragile prompt only works on simple sentences.

## 🔗 Analogy

A sturdy umbrella - it works in light rain, heavy rain, wind, and even hail. A fragile one breaks in wind. Robust prompts work across all conditions; fragile ones break on unusual inputs.
