# Specificity

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The more specific your prompt, the more targeted the response. Specificity means giving exact details about what you want.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Vague: 'Write a story.' Specific: 'Write a 500-word science fiction story about a Hong Kong student who discovers their phone is an AI from 2050. Include a plot twist. Rating: PG.'

## 🔗 Analogy

Ordering at a cha chaan teng - 'I want food' (vague) vs 'I want a pineapple bun with butter, a hot milk tea, less sugar, and a scrambled egg sandwich' (specific). You get exactly what you want.
