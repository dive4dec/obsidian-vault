# Underprompting

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Being too vague gives the model too much freedom, often producing generic or unhelpful responses.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Bad: 'Help me with Python.' The model doesn't know if you want a tutorial, code fix, concept explanation, or project idea. It guesses and may miss your need entirely.

## 🔗 Analogy

Asking a librarian 'I need a book' - without genre, topic, or level, they guess randomly. You might get a children's book or a PhD thesis. Underprompting leaves too much to chance.
