# Zero-Shot Prompting

> **Domain:** [[_prompt-engineering-moc|Prompt Engineering]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Asking the model to do a task without providing examples. Large, well-tuned models can often do this.

## 📋 Concrete Example

'Classify this email as spam or not spam: CONGRATULATIONS YOU WON $1000000' - No examples needed. The model just classifies it as `spam`.

## 🔗 Analogy

Asking a fluent speaker to translate a sentence - you don't need to show them examples first. They just know how to translate. Zero-shot relies on the model's pre-existing capabilities.
