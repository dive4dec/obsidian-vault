# Accountability

> **Domain:** [[_agent-fundamentals-moc|Agent Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

When an agent takes action, someone must be responsible. Accountability ensures humans can trace actions to decisions and actors.

## 📋 Concrete Example

If an agent sends an incorrect email to 1000 customers, who is responsible? The developer who built it? The user who triggered it? Accountability frameworks clarify responsibility.

## 🔗 Analogy

A company org chart - if a bad decision is made, the chart shows who made it and who approved it. Accountability for agents means tracing actions back to the humans responsible.
