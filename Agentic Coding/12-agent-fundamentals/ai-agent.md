# AI Agent

> **Domain:** [[_agent-fundamentals-moc|Agent Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

An AI that just answers questions is limited. An agent can take actions - use tools, make decisions, pursue goals - autonomously.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A chatbot answers 'How is the weather?' with text. An agent checks a weather API, sees it will rain, and reminds you to bring an umbrella - it took action.

## 🔗 Analogy

A receptionist who not only answers questions but also books appointments, sends reminders, and orders lunch. They do things, not just talk.
