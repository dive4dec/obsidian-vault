# Goal-Oriented

> **Domain:** [[_agent-fundamentals-moc|Agent Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

An agent is given a goal (not just a question) and works toward it, potentially over many steps.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Goal: 'Find the cheapest flight to Tokyo next Friday.' The agent searches multiple sites, compares prices, finds the best deal, and presents it. It pursues the goal over multiple steps.

## 🔗 Analogy

A personal shopper - you say 'I need a gift for my mom under $200.' They browse, compare, pick something, wrap it. They work toward your goal, not just answer one question.
