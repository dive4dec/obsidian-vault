# Human-in-the-Loop

> **Domain:** [[_agent-fundamentals-moc|Agent Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Some agent actions require human approval before execution. This keeps humans in control of important decisions.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Agent drafts an email but does NOT send it. Human reviews and clicks `Send.` The agent does the work; the human makes the final call on important actions.

## 🔗 Analogy

A co-pilot on a plane - the autopilot flies, but the human pilot handles takeoff, landing, and emergencies. The agent is the autopilot; the human is the co-pilot who takes over at critical moments.
