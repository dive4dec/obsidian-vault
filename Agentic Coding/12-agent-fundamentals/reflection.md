# Reflection

> **Domain:** [[_agent-fundamentals-moc|Agent Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

After completing a task, the agent reflects on what went well, what failed, and what to improve. This metacognition improves future performance.

## 📋 Concrete Example

After a failed coding attempt: 'The error was a type mismatch. I should have checked types first. Next time, I will add type checking before running.' The reflection improves future attempts.

## 🔗 Analogy

A player reviewing game footage - they watch their mistakes, identify what to improve, and play better next time. Reflection is the agent reviewing its own 'game footage.'
