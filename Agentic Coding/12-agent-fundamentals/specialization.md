# Specialization

> **Domain:** [[_agent-fundamentals-moc|Agent Fundamentals]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Specialized agents are better at their specific task than a general-purpose agent. Division of labor improves overall performance.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A general agent might code at 70% quality. A specialized coding agent codes at 90%. A specialized testing agent tests at 90%. Together, they outperform one general agent doing everything at 70%.

## 🔗 Analogy

A specialized factory vs a general workshop - the factory's assembly line (specialized agents) produces cars faster and better than one person building a whole car alone. Specialization improves efficiency.
