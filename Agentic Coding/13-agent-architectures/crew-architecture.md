# Crew Architecture

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architectures-moc|Agent Architectures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A team of agents with defined roles working together, like a film crew. Each member has a specific job.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Crew: Captain (planner), Researcher (info gatherer), Engineer (coder), QA (tester), Reviewer (quality checker). The Captain coordinates; each member does their part.

## 🔗 Analogy

A film production crew - director (captain), camera operator, sound engineer, lighting, makeup. Each has a role. Together they make a film. Crew architecture gives agents defined crew roles.
