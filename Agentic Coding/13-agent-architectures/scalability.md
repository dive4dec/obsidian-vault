# Scalability

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architectures-moc|Agent Architectures]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The ability to handle more work by adding more agents or resources. A scalable architecture grows without breaking.

## 📋 Concrete Example

10 tasks: 1 agent finishes in 100s. 100 tasks: add 9 more agents, finish in 100s (not 1000s). The architecture scales horizontally by adding more agents.

## 🔗 Analogy

A scalable restaurant - 10 customers: 1 chef handles it. 100 customers: add 9 more chefs. Each chef works independently. The restaurant scales by adding more chefs, not by making one chef work 10x faster.
