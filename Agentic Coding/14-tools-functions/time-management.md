# Time Management

> **Domain:** [[_tools-function-calling-moc|Tools & Function Calling]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The agent prioritizes tasks, allocates time, and avoids spending too long on any single step.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Agent: 'I have 30 minutes. Testing takes 10, debugging takes 15, deployment takes 5. I will skip documentation for now.' It manages time within constraints.

## 🔗 Analogy

Budgeting your study time - 2 hours total: 45 min math (hardest), 30 min English, 30 min science, 15 min break. Time management lets agents budget their time wisely.
