# Knowledge Update

> **Domain:** [[_memory-state-moc|Memory & State]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Refreshing the agent's knowledge with new facts, corrections, or updated information over time.

## 📋 Concrete Example

January: 'Python 3.12 is latest.' August: 'Python 3.13 released.' Update knowledge. The agent stays current by updating its stored facts.

## 🔗 Analogy

Updating a textbook - a science textbook from 2010 says Pluto is a planet. A 2025 edition says it is not. Knowledge updates keep the agent's facts current.
