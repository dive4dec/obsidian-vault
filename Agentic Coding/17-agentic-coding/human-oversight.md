# Human Oversight

> **Domain:** [[_agentic-coding-moc|Agentic Coding]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Even autonomous agents need human oversight: reviewing code, approving deployments, setting boundaries.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Agent: 'I have written the code, tests pass, ready to deploy. Please review and approve.' Human: reviews code, checks for security, approves. The human is the final checkpoint.

## 🔗 Analogy

A co-pilot on a plane - the autopilot flies, but the co-pilot monitors instruments and takes over if needed. Human oversight ensures the agent stays on track.
