# Accountability in AI

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

When AI makes a mistake, someone must be held accountable. Clear accountability frameworks are essential.

## 📋 Concrete Example

AI makes a wrong medical diagnosis. Who is responsible? The doctor who relied on it? The AI developer? The hospital? Accountability frameworks clarify responsibility.

## 🔗 Analogy

A delivery company's policy - if a package is damaged, the company (not the customer) is accountable. Accountability in AI defines who is responsible for AI mistakes.
