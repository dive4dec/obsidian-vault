# AI Incident Reporting

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

System for reporting, tracking, and learning from AI incidents. Like aviation incident reporting for flight safety.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Report: 'AI gave wrong medical advice. Patient affected. Root cause: outdated training data. Fix: update data, add disclaimer.' Shared with community to prevent similar incidents.

## 🔗 Analogy

Aviation incident reports - pilots report near-misses and problems. The aviation community learns from each report. AI incident reporting does this for AI safety.
