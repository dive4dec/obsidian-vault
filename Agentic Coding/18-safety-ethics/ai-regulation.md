# AI Regulation

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Laws and regulations governing AI development and use. The EU AI Act, US executive orders, and China's AI rules are examples.

## 📋 Concrete Example

EU AI Act: high-risk AI (medical, hiring) must be transparent, tested, and human-overseen. Banned: social scoring, real-time biometric surveillance. Regulations set the rules.

## 🔗 Analogy

Traffic laws for cars - speed limits, seatbelt requirements, license needed. AI regulation sets similar rules for AI development and use.
