# Anonymization

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Removing personally identifiable information from data before using it for training.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Before training: 'Alice Smith, 16, Hong Kong, likes Python.' After anonymization: 'Student A, ~16, Asia, likes coding.' Identity removed, useful patterns kept.

## 🔗 Analogy

Removing name tags from uniforms before donating - the new owner does not know who wore it. Anonymization removes identity from data.
