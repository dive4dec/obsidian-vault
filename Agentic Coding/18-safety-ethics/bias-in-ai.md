# Bias in AI

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI systems can reflect and amplify human biases - racial, gender, cultural. Bias detection and mitigation are essential.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A hiring AI that favors male names over female names is biased. Cause: training data had more male hires. Fix: balanced training data, fairness metrics, regular audits.

## 🔗 Analogy

A judge who is stricter with people from certain backgrounds - that is bias. AI bias is the same: the system treats some groups unfairly. Detecting and fixing bias is essential.
