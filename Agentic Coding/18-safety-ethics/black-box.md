# Black Box

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

When the AI's decision-making is opaque - you cannot see inside. Deep learning models are often black boxes.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Neural network: input goes in, output comes out, but the 175B parameters in between are a mystery. You cannot easily explain why it made a specific decision.

## 🔗 Analogy

A vending machine - you put in money and get a snack, but you cannot see the internal mechanism. It works, but you do not know how. Black box AI works but does not explain how.
