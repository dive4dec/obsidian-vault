# Consent

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Users should consent to their data being used by AI. Consent must be informed, specific, and revocable.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Consent: 'I agree that my conversation data may be used to improve the AI.' The user must understand what they are agreeing to and be able to withdraw consent.

## 🔗 Analogy

Permission slips for field trips - parents must sign (consent) before the school takes their child on a trip. AI consent means users agree to how their data is used.
