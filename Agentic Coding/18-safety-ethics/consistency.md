# Consistency

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The AI gives similar answers to similar questions. Inconsistent AI is confusing and untrustworthy.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Consistency: 'What is 2+2?' -> '4'. 'What is two plus two?' -> '4'. 'What is 2 + 2?' -> '4'. Same answer regardless of phrasing.

## 🔗 Analogy

A consistent referee - the same foul gets the same penalty every time. Consistency in AI means similar inputs produce similar outputs.
