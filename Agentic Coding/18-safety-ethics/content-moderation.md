# Content Moderation

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Automatically detecting and filtering harmful, offensive, or inappropriate content in AI outputs.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Moderation API: input 'hate speech text' -> output: {hate: 0.95, violence: 0.02, sexual: 0.01}. If hate > 0.7, block the content. Automated filtering at scale.

## 🔗 Analogy

A bouncer at a club - they check each person (content) and refuse entry to troublemakers (harmful content). Content moderation is the bouncer for AI outputs.
