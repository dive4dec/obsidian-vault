# Copyright

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI trained on copyrighted data raises legal questions: does the output infringe copyright? Who owns AI-generated content?

## 📋 Concrete Example

Issue: AI generates an image in the style of a living artist. Is this copyright infringement? Legal systems are still working this out. Transparency about training data helps.

## 🔗 Analogy

Sampling in music - if you use a piece of someone else's song, you need permission and must pay royalties. AI training on copyrighted data raises similar questions.
