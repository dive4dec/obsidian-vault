# Data Privacy

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Personal data (name, ID, location, health) must be protected. AI should not leak or expose this information.

## 📋 Concrete Example

The AI must not: 'Alice Smith, ID A123456, lives at 123 Main St.' Even if it learned this from training data, it should not reveal it. Data privacy protects personal information.

## 🔗 Analogy

A doctor keeping medical records confidential - your health information is private. Data privacy in AI means personal information stays protected.
