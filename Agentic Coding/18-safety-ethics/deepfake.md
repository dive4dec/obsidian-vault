# Deepfake

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI-generated fake images, audio, or video that look real. Can be used for misinformation, fraud, or harassment.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Deepfake: a video of a politician saying something they never said. Looks real. Can influence elections, ruin reputations, or enable scams.

## 🔗 Analogy

A counterfeit painting - it looks like a Picasso but is fake. Deepfakes are counterfeit media: they look real but are fabricated.
