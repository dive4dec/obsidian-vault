# Differential Privacy

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Adding mathematical noise to data so the AI learns patterns without memorizing any individual's data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

With differential privacy, the AI learns 'the average age is 25' but cannot determine any specific person's age. Patterns are preserved, individuals are protected.

## 🔗 Analogy

A blurred photo - you can see the general scene (patterns) but cannot identify faces (individuals). Differential privacy blurs data to protect individuals.
