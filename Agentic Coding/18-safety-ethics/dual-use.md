# Dual Use

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Technology that can be used for both good and harm. AI is dual-use: it can cure diseases or create bioweapons.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Dual use: AI that discovers new medicines could also design toxic chemicals. AI that writes code could also write malware. Managing dual-use risks is essential.

## 🔗 Analogy

A kitchen knife - it can chop vegetables (good) or harm someone (bad). The knife is dual-use. AI is the same: helpful or harmful depending on the user.
