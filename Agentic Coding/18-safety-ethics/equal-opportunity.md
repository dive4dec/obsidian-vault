# Equal Opportunity

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A fairness metric: among qualified candidates, the AI should select at similar rates across groups.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Of qualified men, 80% are selected. Of qualified women, 80% are selected. Equal opportunity. If 80% of men but 60% of women, there is unequal opportunity.

## 🔗 Analogy

A scholarship committee - among equally qualified students, the selection rate should be the same regardless of background. Equal opportunity checks this for AI.
