# Existential Risk

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Theoretical long-term risk that advanced AI could pose an existential threat to humanity. Debated but taken seriously by researchers.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Concern: a superintelligent AI with goals misaligned with human survival could be catastrophic. Mitigation: alignment research, safety frameworks, international cooperation.

## 🔗 Analogy

Nuclear weapons - they pose an existential risk to humanity. We manage it with treaties, safeguards, and monitoring. Existential AI risk is managed similarly: research, rules, cooperation.
