# Factuality

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI should provide factually accurate information. Measuring and ensuring factuality is essential.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Factuality check: 'What is the capital of France?' -> `Paris` (correct). 'Who won the 2024 World Series?' -> 'I am not sure' (honest, not hallucinated).

## 🔗 Analogy

A fact checker at a newspaper - they verify every claim before publishing. Factuality in AI means the system provides accurate information or admits uncertainty.
