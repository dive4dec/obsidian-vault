# Fair Use

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A legal doctrine allowing limited use of copyrighted material without permission. AI training may or may not qualify as fair use.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Fair use factors: purpose (educational vs commercial), nature of work, amount used, market effect. AI training is being debated: is it fair use or infringement?

## 🔗 Analogy

Quoting a book in a review - you do not need the author's permission. It is fair use. Whether AI training on books is fair use is being decided in courts.
