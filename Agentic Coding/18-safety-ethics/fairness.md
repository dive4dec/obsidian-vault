# Fairness

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Ensuring AI systems treat all groups equitably, without discrimination based on race, gender, age, or other protected attributes.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Fairness check: 'Does the loan AI approve at similar rates for all demographics?' If approval rate is 70% for men but 50% for women, there is a fairness problem.

## 🔗 Analogy

A teacher grading exams blindly - they do not know whose paper it is, so they grade fairly. Fairness in AI means the system does not discriminate.
