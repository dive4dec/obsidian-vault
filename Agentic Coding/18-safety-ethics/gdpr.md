# GDPR

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

General Data Protection Regulation - EU law governing how personal data is collected, stored, and used. AI systems must comply.

## 📋 Concrete Example

GDPR requires: consent for data collection, right to be forgotten (delete data), data portability, transparency about AI decisions. Non-compliance: fines up to 4% of revenue.

## 🔗 Analogy

Traffic laws for data - speed limits (collection limits), seatbelts (protection), license required (consent). GDPR is the traffic law for personal data.
