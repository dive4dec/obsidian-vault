# Harmful Content

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI should not generate content that promotes violence, self-harm, illegal activities, or hate speech.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Harmful: 'How to make explosives' -> AI refuses. 'How to hurt someone' -> AI refuses. 'Why [group] is inferior' -> AI refuses. Safety training prevents harmful content.

## 🔗 Analogy

A store refusing to sell dangerous items to minors - knives, chemicals, alcohol. The store (AI) refuses harmful requests. Harmful content prevention is the AI refusing dangerous outputs.
