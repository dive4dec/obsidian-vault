# Interpretability

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

How easy it is for a human to understand the AI's internal decision-making process. More interpretable = more trustworthy.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Simple decision tree: 'If age > 18 and income > 30K, approve loan.' Very interpretable. Deep neural network with 175B parameters: hard to interpret. Trade-off between performance and interpretability.

## 🔗 Analogy

A clear recipe vs a magic black box - the recipe (interpretable) shows each step. The black box (uninterpretable) gives results but not reasons. Interpretability is the recipe.
