# Misinformation

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI can generate false information that looks credible. Preventing misinformation is a key safety challenge.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Risk: AI generates a convincing but fake news article. It looks real. Prevention: fact-checking, RAG for grounding, source citation, human review for sensitive topics.

## 🔗 Analogy

A rumor that spreads in school - it sounds true but is made up. Misinformation from AI is a digital rumor that looks credible but is false.
