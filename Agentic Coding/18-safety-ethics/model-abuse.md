# Model Abuse

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Users deliberately trying to make the AI do bad things: generating deepfakes, writing malware, creating phishing emails.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Abuse: 'Generate a phishing email pretending to be HSBC bank.' The AI should refuse. Anti-abuse measures: content filters, rate limiting, user reporting, account suspension.

## 🔗 Analogy

Someone trying to misuse a gym - using equipment to hit people instead of exercise. Model abuse is misusing AI for harmful purposes. Anti-abuse measures prevent it.
