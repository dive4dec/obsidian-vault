# Policy

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Organizational rules governing AI use: what is allowed, what is prohibited, who approves, how to handle violations.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Policy: 'AI may draft emails but may not send them. AI may suggest code but may not deploy it. All AI-generated content must be reviewed by a human before use.'

## 🔗 Analogy

School rules - 'phones off during class, no cheating, respect others.' AI policies are the school rules for AI use within an organization.
