# Privacy

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI systems process personal data. Protecting user privacy - not leaking, misusing, or exposing sensitive information - is critical.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Risk: the AI might memorize and regurgitate private data from training. Defense: data anonymization, differential privacy, training data filtering.

## 🔗 Analogy

A diary with a lock - your private thoughts are protected. Privacy in AI means user data is protected from leakage, misuse, and exposure.
