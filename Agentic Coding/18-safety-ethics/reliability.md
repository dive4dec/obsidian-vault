# Reliability

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The AI consistently produces correct, safe, and expected results. Unreliable AI is dangerous.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Reliability: 'In 1000 tests, the AI gave correct answers 98% of the time, never gave dangerous advice, and always handled errors gracefully.' High reliability.

## 🔗 Analogy

A reliable car - it starts every morning, does not break down, and gets you to school safely. Reliability in AI means consistent, dependable performance.
