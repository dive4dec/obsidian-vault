# Right to be Forgotten

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Users can request their data be deleted from AI systems. The AI must be able to remove individual data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

User: 'Delete all my data.' System: removes their profile, conversation history, and personal information. The right to be forgotten gives users control.

## 🔗 Analogy

Erasing someone from a photo - you remove them completely. The right to be forgotten erases a person's data from the AI system.
