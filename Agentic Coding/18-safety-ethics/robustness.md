# Robustness

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The AI handles unusual, unexpected, or adversarial inputs without failing. Robust systems degrade gracefully.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Robust: handles empty input, very long input, foreign languages, code injection attempts, and nonsensical questions. Each is handled appropriately, not with a crash.

## 🔗 Analogy

A sturdy bridge - it handles cars, trucks, wind, rain, and even earthquakes. Robust AI handles all kinds of inputs without breaking.
