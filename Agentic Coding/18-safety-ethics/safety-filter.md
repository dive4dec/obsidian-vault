# Safety Filter

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Software that checks AI inputs and outputs for harmful content, blocking or modifying dangerous responses.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Filter checks output: 'Here is how to make...' (weapon instructions). Filter blocks: 'I cannot provide that information.' The safety filter prevents harmful content from reaching users.

## 🔗 Analogy

A water filter - it removes impurities before the water reaches your glass. A safety filter removes harmful content before it reaches the user.
