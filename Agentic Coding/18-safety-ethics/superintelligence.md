# Superintelligence

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Hypothetical AI that surpasses human intelligence in all domains. Raises unique safety and ethical questions.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Superintelligence could: solve climate change, cure all diseases, but also might be uncontrollable if not aligned. Theoretical but drives current safety research.

## 🔗 Analogy

A chess computer that always beats grandmasters is superintelligent at chess. A general superintelligence would exceed humans at everything. Safety research prepares for this possibility.
