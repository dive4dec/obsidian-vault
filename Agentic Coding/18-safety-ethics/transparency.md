# Transparency

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

AI systems should be transparent about how they work, what data they use, and why they make certain decisions.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Transparency: 'This AI was trained on public web data. It uses a transformer model. Its decisions are based on patterns in that data. It may make mistakes.' Open about capabilities and limitations.

## 🔗 Analogy

Nutrition labels on food - they tell you exactly what is inside. Transparency in AI means telling users what is inside the AI: data, methods, limitations.
