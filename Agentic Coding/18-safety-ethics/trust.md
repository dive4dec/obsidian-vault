# Trust

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Users must trust AI systems to use them. Trust is built through transparency, consistency, reliability, and safety.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Trust factors: 'The AI is accurate (90%), explains its decisions (transparency), handles errors gracefully (reliability), and protects my data (safety).' All factors build trust.

## 🔗 Analogy

Trusting a friend - you trust them because they are honest (transparency), reliable (consistency), helpful (reliability), and keep your secrets (safety). Trust in AI is built the same way.
