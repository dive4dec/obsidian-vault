# Watermark

> **Domain:** [[_safety-ethics-moc|Safety & Ethics]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Embedding a hidden marker in AI-generated content to distinguish it from human-created content.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Watermark: a subtle pattern in AI-generated images that says `AI-generated` when detected. Helps identify deepfakes and AI content.

## 🔗 Analogy

A watermark on paper - hold it up to light and you see the brand. AI watermarks are hidden marks that identify content as AI-generated.
