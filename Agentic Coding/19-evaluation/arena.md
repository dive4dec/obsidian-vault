# Arena

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A platform where users ask questions, two anonymous models answer, and users vote on the better answer. Live leaderboard.

## 📋 Concrete Example

LMSYS Arena: you ask a question. Model A and Model B both answer (you do not know which is which). You vote. Rankings update in real-time. Crowd-sourced evaluation.

## 🔗 Analogy

A blind taste test at a food fair - you try two dishes without knowing the chefs, and vote for the better one. Arenas are blind taste tests for AI models.
