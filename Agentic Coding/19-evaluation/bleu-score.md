# BLEU Score

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Bilingual Evaluation Understudy - measures how close machine translation is to human translation. N-gram overlap.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Translation: 'The cat sits' vs reference 'The cat is sitting.' BLEU measures n-gram overlap. Higher BLEU = closer to human translation.

## 🔗 Analogy

A teacher comparing your translation to the answer key - how many words match? How many phrases match? BLEU scores translation quality similarly.
