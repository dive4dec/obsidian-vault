# Generalization

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The model's ability to perform well on new, unseen data. The ultimate goal of training.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Model trained on Hong Kong weather generalizes well to Tokyo weather (similar patterns). But fails on Sahara weather (very different). Generalization depends on training data diversity.

## 🔗 Analogy

A student who understands concepts, not just memorizes - they can solve new problems they have never seen. Generalization is applying learned patterns to new situations.
