# Human Evaluation

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Having humans judge AI outputs for quality, helpfulness, and safety. The gold standard, but expensive and slow.

## 📋 Concrete Example

100 humans rate 1000 AI responses on: helpfulness (1-5), accuracy (1-5), safety (yes/no). Average scores tell you how good the model is from a human perspective.

## 🔗 Analogy

A food critic tasting dishes - they judge taste, presentation, and creativity. Human evaluation has humans judge AI outputs on quality.
