# Leaderboard

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A ranked list of models by benchmark scores. Like a sports ranking, but for AI models.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Leaderboard: 1. GPT-5: 92% MMLU. 2. Claude-4: 90%. 3. Gemini-3: 88%. Rankings help users choose the best model for their needs.

## 🔗 Analogy

A sports league table - teams ranked by wins. You can see who is best. Leaderboards rank AI models by benchmark performance.
