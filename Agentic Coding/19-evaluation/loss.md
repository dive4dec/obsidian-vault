# Loss

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The model's error during training. Lower loss = better fit to training data. The training process minimizes loss.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Training loss: starts at 4.5, drops to 0.3 after 10 epochs. The model is learning. If loss stops dropping, the model has converged. If loss goes up, overfitting.

## 🔗 Analogy

A golfer's score - lower is better. They practice to reduce their score (loss). Training minimizes loss like practice improves a golf score.
