# Overfitting

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The model memorizes training data but cannot generalize to new data. Like a student who memorizes answers without understanding.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Overfit model: 99% on training data, 60% on test data. It memorized specific examples but cannot solve new problems. Prevention: regularization, more data, early stopping.

## 🔗 Analogy

A student who memorizes past exam answers - they score 100% on old exams but fail the real exam with new questions. Overfitting is memorizing without understanding.
