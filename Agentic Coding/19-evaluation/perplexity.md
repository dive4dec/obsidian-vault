# Perplexity

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

How surprised the model is by the next token. Lower perplexity = better prediction = better model (on language tasks).

## 📋 Concrete Example

Perplexity on a text: 3.2 (good - model predicted most words correctly) vs 15.7 (poor - model was surprised by many words). Lower is better.

## 🔗 Analogy

A student's confidence in predicting the next word in a sentence - 'The cat sat on the...' If they guess `mat` confidently, low perplexity. If they cannot guess, high perplexity.
