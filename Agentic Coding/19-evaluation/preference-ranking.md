# Preference Ranking

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Humans compare two AI outputs and pick the better one. Used for RLHF training and evaluation.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Which is better? A: 'Python is a programming language.' B: 'Python is a high-level, interpreted programming language known for readability.' Humans pick B (more informative). Rankings train the model.

## 🔗 Analogy

A taste test between two colas - people pick the one they prefer. Preference ranking has humans pick the better AI response.
