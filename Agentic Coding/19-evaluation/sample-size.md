# Sample Size

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

How many test examples used. Larger sample sizes give more reliable results.

## 📋 Concrete Example

10 examples: result could be lucky. 100 examples: more reliable. 10,000 examples: very reliable. But 10,000 examples cost more time and money. Balance reliability with cost.

## 🔗 Analogy

A survey - asking 10 people vs 1000 people. 1000 gives a more reliable result. Sample size determines how trustworthy evaluation results are.
