# Underfitting

> **Domain:** [[_evaluation-moc|Evaluation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The model is too simple to capture the patterns in the data. Performs poorly on both training and test data.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Underfit model: 60% on training data, 55% on test data. It has not learned enough. Fix: more complex model, more training, better features.

## 🔗 Analogy

A student who did not study enough - they score 55% on both practice and real exams. They have not learned the material. Underfitting is not learning enough.
