# Annotation

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The process of labeling data, often by human experts. Quality annotation is expensive but crucial.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Annotation: 5 experts read 10,000 texts and label each as 'positive,' 'neutral,' or `negative.` Inter-annotator agreement: 92%. High-quality labels.

## 🔗 Analogy

A museum curator labeling artifacts - each artifact gets a card with its name, era, and description. Annotation labels data for the model.
