# Clustering

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Grouping similar items without labels. An unsupervised learning technique.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Clustering: 1000 news articles -> 5 groups (sports, politics, tech, entertainment, health). The model found these groups without being told the categories.

## 🔗 Analogy

Sorting laundry - you naturally separate whites, colors, and delicates. No labels needed - you see similarity. Clustering groups items by similarity.
