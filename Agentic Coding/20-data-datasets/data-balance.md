# Data Balance

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Having equal or proportional representation across categories. Imbalanced data causes biased models.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Balance: 50% cat images, 50% dog images. Balanced. vs 90% cat, 10% dog. Imbalanced - model will be biased toward cats. Balance ensures fair training.

## 🔗 Analogy

A balanced diet - equal portions of protein, vegetables, and carbs. Data balance ensures the model learns all categories equally.
