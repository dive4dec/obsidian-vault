# Data Distribution

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

How data is spread across categories, values, or time. Understanding distribution prevents bias.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Distribution: 80% of training data is about Western culture, 20% rest of world. The model will be biased toward Western knowledge. Distribution matters.

## 🔗 Analogy

A classroom demographic - 30 boys and 5 girls. The class is skewed. Data distribution shows how data is spread, revealing potential biases.
