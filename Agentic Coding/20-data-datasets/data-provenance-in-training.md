# Data Provenance in Training

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Tracking where every piece of training data came from: source, date, modifications, license. Essential for compliance.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Provenance: 'Article 1234: source: BBC News, date: 2024-03-15, original, license: CC-BY, no modifications.' Full traceability for every training example.

## 🔗 Analogy

A diamond's certificate - mine of origin, cut date, carat weight, chain of ownership. Data provenance is the certificate for each training example.
