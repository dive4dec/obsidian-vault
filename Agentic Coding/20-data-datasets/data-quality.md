# Data Quality

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

How good the data is: accurate, complete, diverse, relevant, unbiased. Better data = better models.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Quality check: 95% accurate labels, 0% duplicates, diverse topics, no bias, relevant to task. High quality. vs 60% accurate labels, 20% duplicates, biased. Low quality.

## 🔗 Analogy

Ingredients for cooking - fresh, high-quality ingredients make better dishes. High-quality data makes better models. Data quality is the freshness of the ingredients.
