# Data Sampling

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Selecting a subset of data for training or evaluation. Different sampling strategies for different needs.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Sampling: from 1M articles, randomly select 100K for training. Or stratified sample: ensure equal representation from each category. Strategy matters.

## 🔗 Analogy

Taking a spoonful of soup to taste - you sample a small part to represent the whole. Data sampling selects representative subsets for training.
