# Reinforcement Learning

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Learning by trial and error. The model gets rewards for good actions and penalties for bad ones.

## 📋 Concrete Example

A game-playing AI: it tries moves, gets points (rewards) or loses points (penalties). Over millions of games, it learns the best strategies. AlphaGo learned this way.

## 🔗 Analogy

Training a dog - correct behavior gets a treat (reward), bad behavior gets nothing (penalty). The dog learns through trial and error. RL trains AI the same way.
