# Stratified Sampling

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Sampling that maintains the proportion of each category. Ensures balanced representation.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Stratified: original data is 60% sports, 40% news. Sample 1000: 600 sports, 400 news. Proportions maintained. No category is underrepresented.

## 🔗 Analogy

A school survey that samples proportionally from each grade - 30% F1, 25% F2, 25% F3, 20% F4. Each grade is fairly represented.
