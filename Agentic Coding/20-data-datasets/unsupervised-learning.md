# Unsupervised Learning

> **Domain:** [[_data-datasets-moc|Data & Datasets]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Finding patterns in data without labels. The model discovers structure on its own.

## 📋 Concrete Example

10,000 customer profiles, no labels. Model finds: 3 groups (young spenders, budget families, luxury buyers). Clustering - unsupervised pattern discovery.

## 🔗 Analogy

Sorting a mixed bag of Lego bricks by color and shape - no instructions (labels), you just find natural groups. Unsupervised learning finds patterns without labels.
