# Federated Learning

> **Domain:** [[_mlops-moc|MLOps]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Training models across multiple devices without collecting data centrally. Privacy-preserving.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Federated: 1000 phones each train on local data. Only model updates (not data) are sent to server. Server combines updates. Privacy preserved.

## 🔗 Analogy

A study group where each student studies at home, then shares what they learned (not their notes). The group combines insights. Federated learning trains across devices without sharing data.
