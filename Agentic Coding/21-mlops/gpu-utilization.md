# GPU Utilization

> **Domain:** [[_mlops-moc|MLOps]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Maximizing GPU usage during training and inference. Idle GPUs waste money; fully utilized GPUs are efficient.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Utilization: 95% GPU during training (good). 30% GPU during inference (bad - batch more). Monitor and optimize to keep GPUs busy.

## 🔗 Analogy

A factory running at 95% capacity - machines are busy, efficient. At 30%, machines sit idle, wasting money. GPU utilization keeps GPUs busy.
