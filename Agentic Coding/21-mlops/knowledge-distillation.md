# Knowledge Distillation

> **Domain:** [[_mlops-moc|MLOps]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A small student model learns from a large teacher model. Student is small but inherits teacher's knowledge.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Teacher: 175B parameters, 92% accuracy. Student: 7B parameters, 85% accuracy. Student learned from teacher's outputs. Small, fast, decent accuracy.

## 🔗 Analogy

A master teaching an apprentice - the master (teacher) has deep knowledge. The apprentice (student) learns from the master. Smaller but capable.
