# Model Optimization

> **Domain:** [[_mlops-moc|MLOps]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Making models faster, smaller, and cheaper to run. Includes quantization, pruning, distillation, and architectural improvements.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Optimization plan: 1) Quantize to 8-bit (-75% size). 2) Prune 30% neurons (-30% size). 3) Distill to smaller model (-90% size). Total: 96% smaller, 3% accuracy loss.

## 🔗 Analogy

Tuning a car for racing - lighter parts, better aerodynamics, optimized engine. Same car, much faster. Model optimization makes models faster and cheaper.
