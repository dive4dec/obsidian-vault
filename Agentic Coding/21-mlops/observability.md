# Observability

> **Domain:** [[_mlops-moc|MLOps]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Monitoring not just performance but internal model behavior: attention patterns, activation distributions, error patterns.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Observability: 'Layer 5 activations are unusually high on certain inputs.' This insight helps debug why the model makes specific errors. Deep visibility.

## 🔗 Analogy

An X-ray for a patient - you see inside the body, not just external symptoms. Observability sees inside the model, not just input-output.
