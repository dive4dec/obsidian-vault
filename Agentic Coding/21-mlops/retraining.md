# Retraining

> **Domain:** [[_mlops-moc|MLOps]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Training the model again with new data. Essential when drift is detected or new data is available.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Retraining: monthly. New data from the past month is added. Model retrains. New version: v4 (Jul, 92%). Deployed. Performance maintained.

## 🔗 Analogy

A student reviewing new material each month - they learn new things and update their knowledge. Retraining updates the model with new data.
