# Account Settings

> **Domain:** [[_dsh-configuration-moc|Configuration]]

## Motivation

The Account settings section (`dsh-client-ui-settings-account`) displays DeepSeek login state and offers browser sign-in and cancellation; the sidebar account menu provides Platform sign-out. Desktop users also receive a resumable introduction to account credit and presentation preferences.

## Concrete Example

Open the Account section, sign in through the system browser; the resulting grant is stored in the local credential store.

## Analogy

It is the sign-in card in your settings, mirroring the credential store's account records.
