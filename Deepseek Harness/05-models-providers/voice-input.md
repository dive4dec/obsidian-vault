# Voice Input

> **Domain:** [[_dsh-models-providers-moc|Models & Providers]]

## Motivation

`@deepseek-ai/dsh-experimental-voice-input-bundle` is the optional bundle that composes the speech service, the local SenseVoice provider, and microphone control for voice input. Shipped profiles leave it disabled; you enable "Voice Input" in the Web sidebar's Plugins, then record with the microphone between the model selector and Send.

## Concrete Example

Enable Voice Input in the sidebar; if models need preparation a dialog offers "Go to setup" or "Later," then click the microphone and Stop to insert a transcript.

## Analogy

A push-to-talk mic that dictations straight into the composer.
