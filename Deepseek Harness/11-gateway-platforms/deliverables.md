# Deliverables

> **Domain:** [[_dsh-gateways-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

`dsh-client-ui-deliverables` renders the changed-files card a finished turn ends with — the files the turn changed, with the Host's line counts, each opening the turn's review tab on that file — plus cards for explicitly delivered files, and links matching inline-code references in the closing prose. Listed and linked paths come from the recorded summary, successful mutations, and explicit deliveries, never from the prose.

## Concrete Example

A turn that edits two files ends with a changed-files card; clicking a path opens the right Sidebar review tab for that file.

## Analogy

The packing slip on a delivery: it lists exactly what went into the box, not what the driver thought was in it.
