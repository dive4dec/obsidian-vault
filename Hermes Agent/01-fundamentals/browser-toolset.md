# Browser Toolset

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

The browser toolset lets Hermes control a real web browser, clicking buttons and filling forms. This is needed for websites that require login or have dynamic content that simple search cannot reach.

## Concrete Example

Hermes opens a browser, logs into your school portal, and downloads your timetable as a PDF.

## Analogy

Like a robot sitting at a computer, moving the mouse and typing, instead of just reading text.
