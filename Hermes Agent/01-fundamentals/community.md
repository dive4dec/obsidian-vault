# Community

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Hermes has a growing community on GitHub and Discord. You can ask questions, share skills, report bugs, and suggest features. Being part of the community helps you learn faster.

## Concrete Example

You join the Discord server, ask about a setup problem, and someone answers within minutes.

## Analogy

Like joining a school club. You learn from older students and make friends who share your interest.
