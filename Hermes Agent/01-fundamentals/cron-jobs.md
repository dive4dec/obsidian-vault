# Cron Jobs

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Cron jobs are scheduled tasks. You tell Hermes to run something every day at 9am, or every 30 minutes, and it will, even if you are not chatting with it.

## Concrete Example

You set a cron job that checks the weather every morning and messages you on Telegram if a typhoon signal is raised.

## Analogy

Like an alarm clock that does not just ring but also reads you the news.
