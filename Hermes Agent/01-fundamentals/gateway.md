# Gateway

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

The gateway is the part of Hermes that connects to messaging platforms like Telegram and Discord. It runs in the background, receives your messages, and sends Hermes replies back.

## Concrete Example

You start the gateway service. Now when you message your Hermes bot on Telegram, it responds within seconds.

## Analogy

Like a receptionist who answers calls from different phone lines and passes messages to the right person.
