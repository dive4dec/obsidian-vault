# Multi-Platform Agent

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Hermes does not live in just one app. The same agent can talk to you on Telegram, Discord, Slack, WhatsApp, Email, and more. You can start a task on your phone and continue it on your laptop.

## Concrete Example

You message Hermes on Telegram during lunch to start a research task. When you get home, you open your laptop and the results are already saved.

## Analogy

Like Octopus card working on the MTR, buses, ferries, and at 7-Eleven. One card, many places.
