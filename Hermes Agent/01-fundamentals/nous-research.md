# Nous Research

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Nous Research is the AI research lab that builds Hermes Agent. They focus on open, transparent AI that anyone can inspect and improve. Knowing who made the tool helps you trust it.

## Concrete Example

You visit github.com/NousResearch and see the Hermes Agent source code, with thousands of commits and public discussions.

## Analogy

Like knowing which company made your favourite video game, so you can check their updates and community forums.
