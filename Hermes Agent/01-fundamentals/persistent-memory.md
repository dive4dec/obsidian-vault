# Persistent Memory

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Hermes remembers facts about you and your environment across conversations. This means you do not have to reintroduce yourself every time. Memory makes interactions feel continuous.

## Concrete Example

On Monday you tell Hermes your name is Alex. On Friday, Hermes greets you as Alex without being reminded.

## Analogy

Like your school ID card. The school remembers who you are every day, so you do not have to sign in each morning.
