# Provider-Agnostic

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Hermes does not lock you to one AI company. You can switch between OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Google, local models, and more, often without changing your setup. This gives you freedom and saves money.

## Concrete Example

You start using a free DeepSeek model, then switch to a stronger Anthropic model for a harder task, all with one command.

## Analogy

Like choosing different MTR lines to reach the same station. You pick the route that suits you best.
