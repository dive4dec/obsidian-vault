# Reasoning Levels

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Some models can show their thinking steps before answering. Hermes lets you set a reasoning level, from none to extra high. Higher levels take longer but give more thorough answers.

## Concrete Example

You set reasoning to high for a tough maths problem. Hermes writes out each step before giving the final answer.

## Analogy

Like a teacher who shows full working on the board versus one who just writes the answer.
