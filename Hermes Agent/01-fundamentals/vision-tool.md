# Vision Tool

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

The vision tool lets Hermes look at images and understand what is in them. This is useful for reading diagrams, analysing screenshots, or describing photos.

## Concrete Example

You send Hermes a photo of a maths problem on the whiteboard. It reads the equations and solves them.

## Analogy

Like a friend who can look at your drawing and tell you what they see.
