# What Makes Hermes Different

> **Domain:** [[_hermes-fundamentals-moc|Hermes Fundamentals]]

## Motivation

Three things set Hermes apart: it learns from experience (skills), it remembers you across sessions (memory), and it runs on many platforms at once. Most chatbots do none of these.

## Concrete Example

After a week of use, Hermes knows you prefer Python, remembers your school project folder, and can fix bugs in code it helped you write before.

## Analogy

Like a tutor who has taught you for months and knows your strengths, versus a stranger you meet once.
