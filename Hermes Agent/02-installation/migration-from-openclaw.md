# Migration from OpenClaw

> **Domain:** [[_installation-setup-moc|Installation & Setup]]

## Motivation

If you used OpenClaw before, Hermes has a migration command (hermes claw migrate) that imports your settings and sessions so you do not lose your work.

## Concrete Example

You run hermes claw migrate. It reads your old OpenClaw config and copies the compatible settings into Hermes.

## Analogy

Like transferring your phone contacts to a new phone. Your old friends come with you.
