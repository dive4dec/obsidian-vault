# Nous Portal

> **Domain:** [[_installation-setup-moc|Installation & Setup]]

## Motivation

Nous Portal is the official service from Nous Research. One login gives you access to a model and the four Tool Gateway tools (web, image, TTS, browser). It is the easiest way to start.

## Concrete Example

You use hermes setup --portal, log in to Nous Portal, and Hermes is ready to chat, search, draw, and speak.

## Analogy

Like a school all-in-one card: library, canteen, and computer room access in one tap.
