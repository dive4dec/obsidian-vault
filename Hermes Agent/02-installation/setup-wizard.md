# Setup Wizard

> **Domain:** [[_installation-setup-moc|Installation & Setup]]

## Motivation

After installing, the setup wizard (hermes setup) walks you through choosing a model, connecting tools, and setting up messaging. It asks simple questions and configures everything for you.

## Concrete Example

You run hermes setup. It asks which AI company to use, whether you want web search, and whether to connect Telegram. You answer and it saves your choices.

## Analogy

Like the first-time setup of a new phone. It asks your language, Wi-Fi, and account, step by step.
