# Hermes Claw Migrate

> **Domain:** [[_cli-commands-moc|CLI & Commands]]

## Motivation

The hermes claw migrate command imports settings and sessions from OpenClaw, an older tool. If you are moving from OpenClaw to Hermes, this saves your old work.

## Concrete Example

You run hermes claw migrate. It reads your OpenClaw config, copies compatible settings, and imports your session history.

## Analogy

Like a moving company that packs your old house and unpacks it into the new one.
