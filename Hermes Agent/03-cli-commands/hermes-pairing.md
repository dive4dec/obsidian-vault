# Hermes Pairing

> **Domain:** [[_cli-commands-moc|CLI & Commands]]

## Motivation

The hermes pairing command manages DM authorisation. When someone messages your Hermes bot, they must be approved. Subcommands include list, approve, and revoke.

## Concrete Example

A classmate messages your Telegram bot. You run hermes pairing approve alice. Now Alice can chat with your Hermes.

## Analogy

Like approving a friend request. Until you accept, they cannot message you.
