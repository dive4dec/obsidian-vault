# Auth List

> **Domain:** [[_models-providers-moc|Models & Providers]]

## Motivation

List all configured providers and their auth method. Command: hermes auth list.

## Concrete Example

hermes auth list. Output: anthropic (API key), openai (API key), qwen (OAuth), nous (OAuth). Shows all providers and how they authenticate.

## Analogy

Checking your keychain - you see all your keys and what they open. hermes auth list shows all providers and their auth method.
