# Model Quality

> **Domain:** [[_models-providers-moc|Models & Providers]]

## Motivation

How good a model is at reasoning, coding, writing, and following instructions. Varies by provider.

## Concrete Example

Claude: best at coding. GPT-4o: best at general tasks. Gemini: best at long context. DeepSeek: best value. Quality depends on the task.

## Analogy

Teachers with different specialties - math teacher, English teacher, PE teacher. Model quality depends on the task.
