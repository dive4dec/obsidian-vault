# Z.AI GLM

> **Domain:** [[_models-providers-moc|Models & Providers]]

## Motivation

Zhipu AI (Z.AI) models. GLM-4, GLM-5. Known for strong Chinese and English bilingual support.

## Concrete Example

zai/glm-4-plus: excellent for Chinese and English. hermes model set zai/glm-4-plus. Great for Hong Kong students who use both languages.

## Analogy

A bilingual teacher - fluent in both Chinese and English. Z.AI GLM is the bilingual LLM.
