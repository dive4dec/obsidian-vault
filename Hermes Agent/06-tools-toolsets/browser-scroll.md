# Browser Scroll

> **Domain:** [[_tools-toolsets-moc|Tools & Toolsets]]

## Motivation

A tool that scrolls the page up or down to reveal more content.

## Concrete Example

browser_scroll(direction=down). Page scrolls down. More content visible. New snapshot available. Useful for long pages.

## Analogy

Scrolling on your phone - you swipe up to see more. Browser scroll lets Hermes see more of a long page.
