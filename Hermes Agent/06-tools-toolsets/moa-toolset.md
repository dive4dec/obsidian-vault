# Moa Toolset

> **Domain:** [[_tools-toolsets-moc|Tools & Toolsets]]

## Motivation

Mixture of Agents toolset. Sends queries to multiple models and synthesizes results.

## Concrete Example

moa ask: What is 2+2? Claude: 4. GPT-4o: 4. Gemini: 4. Synthesis: 4 (all agree). Multiple models, one answer.

## Analogy

A panel of experts - you ask 3 experts and combine their answers. The moa toolset queries multiple models.
