# Curator Prune

> **Domain:** [[_skills-system-moc|Skills System]]

## Motivation

The curator permanently deletes very old archived skills that have not been restored. Pruning keeps the archive from growing forever.

## Concrete Example

hermes curator prune deletes skills archived over 6 months ago that were never restored. This frees up disk space and keeps the archive manageable.

## Analogy

Throwing out clothes you have not worn in years - you kept them in storage, but eventually you donate them. Pruning permanently removes old archived skills.
