# Skill Customisation

> **Domain:** [[_skills-system-moc|Skills System]]

## Motivation

Editing a skill to fit your specific workflow. Customised skills work better for your particular setup than generic ones.

## Concrete Example

You install a github-pr skill, then customise it: change the PR template to match your team style, add a step to run your specific linter. Now it fits your team perfectly.

## Analogy

Tailoring a suit - you buy one off the rack (install), then the tailor adjusts it to fit you perfectly (customise). Skill customisation tailors a skill to your needs.
