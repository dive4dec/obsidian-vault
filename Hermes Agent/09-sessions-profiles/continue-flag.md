# Continue Flag

> **Domain:** [[_sessions-profiles-moc|Sessions & Profiles]]

## Motivation

A global flag to resume the most recent session. Usage: hermes --continue.

## Concrete Example

hermes --continue chat. Loads the most recent session. No need to remember the ID. Quick way to continue where you left off.

## Analogy

Pressing Continue on a game menu - it loads your last save. --continue loads your last session.
