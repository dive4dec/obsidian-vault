# Resume Flag

> **Domain:** [[_sessions-profiles-moc|Sessions & Profiles]]

## Motivation

A global flag to resume a specific session. Usage: hermes --resume SESSION_ID.

## Concrete Example

hermes --resume a1b2c3d4 chat. Loads session a1b2c3d4. Full history restored. You continue the previous conversation.

## Analogy

A bookmark in a book - you open to the marked page. --resume opens the marked session.
