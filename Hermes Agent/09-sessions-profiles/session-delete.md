# Session Delete

> **Domain:** [[_sessions-profiles-moc|Sessions & Profiles]]

## Motivation

Delete a session. Command: hermes sessions delete --id ID.

## Concrete Example

hermes sessions delete --id a1b2c3d4. Session and all its messages are deleted. Permanent. Frees space.

## Analogy

Deleting a photo from your album - it is gone. Session delete removes a conversation permanently.
