# Session Stats

> **Domain:** [[_sessions-profiles-moc|Sessions & Profiles]]

## Motivation

Show statistics about sessions. Command: hermes sessions stats.

## Concrete Example

hermes sessions stats. Output: Total: 150 sessions. Oldest: 2025-01-15. Newest: 2025-08-04. Total messages: 3,200. Average: 21 msg/session.

## Analogy

A fitness app summary - total steps, average per day. Session stats summarize your conversation history.
