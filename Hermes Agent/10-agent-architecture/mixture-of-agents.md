# Mixture of Agents

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

Instead of one model doing everything, a mixture of agents uses different models for different parts of the task.

## Concrete Example

One model drafts code, another reviews it, and a third writes tests. Together they produce better results.

## Analogy

Like a group project where the writer, artist, and presenter each do what they are best at.
