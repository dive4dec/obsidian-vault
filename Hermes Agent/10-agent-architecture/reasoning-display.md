# Reasoning Display

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

This controls whether the reasoning steps are shown to you or hidden. Showing them helps you learn how the model thinks.

## Concrete Example

With show you see the thinking steps. With hide you only see the final answer.

## Analogy

Like a teacher who either talks through the solution on the board or just writes the final answer.
