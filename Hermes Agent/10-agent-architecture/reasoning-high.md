# Reasoning high

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

Level high gives deep thinking for complex problems that need careful step-by-step reasoning.

## Concrete Example

For a tricky math proof the high level breaks the problem into parts and verifies each step carefully.

## Analogy

Like solving a chess puzzle: you think several moves ahead before deciding.
