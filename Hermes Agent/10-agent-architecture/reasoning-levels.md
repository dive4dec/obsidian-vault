# Reasoning Levels

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

Reasoning levels control how much the model thinks before answering. Higher levels mean more thinking steps for harder problems.

## Concrete Example

Level none gives quick answers. Level medium thinks through steps. Level high spends extra effort on tough math or coding.

## Analogy

Like choosing walking or running: walk for short trips, sprint when you are late for the exam.
