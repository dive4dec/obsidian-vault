# Reasoning low

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

Level low adds a short thinking block. Useful for straightforward tasks that benefit from a moment of planning.

## Concrete Example

Before summarizing a short paragraph the model spends a few seconds planning its answer.

## Analogy

Like checking your homework answers once before handing them in.
