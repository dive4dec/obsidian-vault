# Reasoning minimal

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

Level minimal gives a tiny bit of thinking. Good for easy tasks that need just a quick check.

## Concrete Example

The model does one quick mental step before answering a yes or no question.

## Analogy

Like glancing both ways before crossing a quiet side street.
