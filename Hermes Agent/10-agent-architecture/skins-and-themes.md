# Skins and Themes

> **Domain:** [[_agent-architecture-moc|Agent Architecture]]

## Motivation

Skins change how the Hermes interface looks: colors, layout, and visual style. Themes are preset skin bundles.

## Concrete Example

You pick a dark theme for night study and a light theme for daytime. The layout and colors change instantly.

## Analogy

Like changing your phone wallpaper and icon pack to match your mood.
