# BlueBubbles

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

BlueBubbles is a bridge that lets Hermes send and receive iMessages from Apple devices.

## Concrete Example

You set up BlueBubbles on a Mac and connect Hermes. Now you can iMessage your agent and get replies.

## Analogy

Like a mail forwarding service that sends your letters between two different post offices.
