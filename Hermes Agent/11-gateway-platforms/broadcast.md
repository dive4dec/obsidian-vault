# Broadcast

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Broadcast sends the same message to all connected platforms at once.

## Concrete Example

You broadcast a weather alert. It goes to Telegram, Discord, and Slack simultaneously.

## Analogy

Like a school announcement over the PA system reaching every classroom at once.
