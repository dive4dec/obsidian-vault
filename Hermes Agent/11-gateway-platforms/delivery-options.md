# Delivery Options

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Delivery options control where the agent sends its output: the original chat, local terminal, all platforms, or a specific platform.

## Concrete Example

A cron job can deliver results to the Telegram home channel only, or to all connected platforms.

## Analogy

Like choosing delivery method: standard mail, express, or pickup at the counter.
