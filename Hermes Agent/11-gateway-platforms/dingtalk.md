# DingTalk

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

DingTalk is a business messaging app used in China. Hermes can connect to it for workplace communication.

## Concrete Example

You connect DingTalk and ask Hermes to check tomorrow schedule in the DingTalk chat.

## Analogy

Like having a personal assistant in your work group chat.
