# Discord

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Discord is a chat platform popular with gamers and communities. Hermes can join your Discord server and respond to messages.

## Concrete Example

You add the Hermes bot to your Discord server. In a channel you type a question and the bot answers.

## Analogy

Like having a knowledgeable club member who is always online in your group chat.
