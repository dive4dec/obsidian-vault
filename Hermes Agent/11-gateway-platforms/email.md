# Email

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Hermes can connect to email so you send a message and get a reply in your inbox.

## Concrete Example

You email your agent a question. Hermes reads the email, processes it, and sends a reply back to your inbox.

## Analogy

Like writing to a customer service address and getting a helpful reply the same day.
