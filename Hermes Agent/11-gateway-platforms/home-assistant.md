# Home Assistant

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Home Assistant is a smart home platform. Hermes can connect to control lights, sensors, and devices.

## Concrete Example

You ask Hermes through Telegram to turn on the living room light. The gateway sends the command to Home Assistant.

## Analogy

Like a remote control for your whole house that you operate through chat.
