# Home Channels

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Home channels are the primary chat locations where the agent sends notifications and updates.

## Concrete Example

You set your Telegram chat as a home channel. Cron job results and alerts go there automatically.

## Analogy

Like your home address: important mail always gets delivered there.
