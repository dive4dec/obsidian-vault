# Platform Adapter

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

A platform adapter translates messages between a specific platform format and the Hermes internal format.

## Concrete Example

The Telegram adapter converts Telegram JSON into Hermes messages and Hermes replies back into Telegram format.

## Analogy

Like a translator at a conference who converts between two languages so both sides understand.
