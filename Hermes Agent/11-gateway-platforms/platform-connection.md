# Platform Connection

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

A platform connection is an active link between Hermes and a messaging platform, with credentials and settings.

## Concrete Example

You create a Telegram connection with your bot token. Once active, messages flow both ways.

## Analogy

Like a phone line: once connected, you can call and receive calls.
