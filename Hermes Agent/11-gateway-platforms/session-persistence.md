# Session Persistence

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Session persistence keeps your conversation context even if the gateway restarts or reconnects.

## Concrete Example

The gateway restarts but your chat history is saved. When it comes back, the agent remembers what you were talking about.

## Analogy

Like saving a video game: when you reload, you continue from where you left off.
