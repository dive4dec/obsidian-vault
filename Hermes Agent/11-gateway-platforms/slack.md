# Slack

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Slack is a workplace messaging tool. Hermes can connect to Slack so you can ask the agent questions at work.

## Concrete Example

You connect Hermes to your Slack workspace. In a direct message you ask it to summarize a document.

## Analogy

Like having a smart colleague on your team chat who can do research for you.
