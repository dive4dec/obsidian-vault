# Telegram

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Telegram is a messaging app that Hermes can connect to. You can chat with your agent from your phone anywhere.

## Concrete Example

You connect Telegram, then send a message to your bot. Hermes replies right in the Telegram chat.

## Analogy

Like texting a friend who happens to be an AI assistant living on your computer.
