# WeCom

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

WeCom is the enterprise version of WeChat. Hermes can connect to it for business messaging.

## Concrete Example

You set up WeCom and the agent joins your company chat. Colleagues can ask it questions.

## Analogy

Like a company intranet assistant available in your work messaging app.
