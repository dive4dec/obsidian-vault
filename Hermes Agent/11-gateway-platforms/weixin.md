# Weixin

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

Weixin (WeChat) is a widely used messaging app in China. Hermes can connect through a bridge to chat on Weixin.

## Concrete Example

You link Weixin and send a voice message. Hermes transcribes it, processes it, and replies in text.

## Analogy

Like having a bilingual friend who translates between you and a foreign pen pal.
