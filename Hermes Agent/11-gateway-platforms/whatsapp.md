# WhatsApp

> **Domain:** [[_gateway-platforms-moc|Gateway & Platforms]]

## Motivation

WhatsApp is a popular messaging app. Hermes can connect through a bridge to reply to your WhatsApp messages.

## Concrete Example

You link WhatsApp and send a message to your own number. Hermes reads it and replies in the same chat.

## Analogy

Like having a pen pal who writes back instantly through your favorite messaging app.
