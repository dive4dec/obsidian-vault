# Cron Delivery Framing

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

The delivery framing wraps the cron job output with context so the message makes sense when it arrives.

## Concrete Example

The message arrives as Cron job weather-check result: Today is 28C and sunny instead of just the raw output.

## Analogy

Like a teacher framing a handout with a title and instructions so students understand what they received.
