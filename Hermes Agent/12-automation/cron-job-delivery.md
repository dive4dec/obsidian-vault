# Cron Job delivery

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

Delivery controls where the cron job result goes: the origin chat, local terminal, all platforms, or a specific one.

## Concrete Example

A cron job set to delivery platform sends its result to Telegram only, not to every connected platform.

## Analogy

Like choosing where to send a photo: to one friend, to a group, or to everyone.
