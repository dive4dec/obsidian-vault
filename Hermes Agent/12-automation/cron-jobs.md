# Cron Jobs

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

Cron jobs are scheduled tasks that run automatically at set times. They let Hermes do things even when you are not chatting.

## Concrete Example

You create a cron job that checks the weather every morning at 7am and sends you a summary on Telegram.

## Analogy

Like an alarm clock that not only rings but also makes your breakfast for you.
