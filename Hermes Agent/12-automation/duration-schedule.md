# Duration Schedule

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

A duration schedule runs a job after a fixed time interval, repeating automatically.

## Concrete Example

You set a duration of 30 minutes. The job runs every 30 minutes: 10:00, 10:30, 11:00, and so on.

## Analogy

Like a bus that comes every 15 minutes on a fixed loop.
