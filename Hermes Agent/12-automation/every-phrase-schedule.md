# Every Phrase Schedule

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

An every phrase schedule uses natural language like every day at 9am or every Monday.

## Concrete Example

You write every Friday at 3pm and the job runs every Friday at 3pm without needing cron syntax.

## Analogy

Like telling a friend call me every Sunday noon in plain words instead of setting a complex alarm.
