# hermes cron list

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

This command shows all your cron jobs, their schedules, and whether they are active.

## Concrete Example

You run hermes cron list and see: weather-check (daily 7am, active), report (weekly Mon 9am, paused).

## Analogy

Like a timetable showing all your classes and whether they are happening or cancelled.
