# hermes cron remove

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

This command permanently deletes a cron job. It cannot be recovered.

## Concrete Example

You run hermes cron remove old-report and the job is gone forever.

## Analogy

Like tearing a page out of your planner: it is gone for good.
