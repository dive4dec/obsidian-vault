# hermes cron resume

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

This command restarts a paused cron job so it runs on its schedule again.

## Concrete Example

After your holiday you run hermes cron resume weather-check and it starts running at 7am again.

## Analogy

Like turning your alarm back on after a break.
