# ISO timestamp schedule

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

An ISO timestamp schedule runs a job once at a specific date and time in ISO format.

## Concrete Example

You write 2026-08-04T14:30:00 to run the job once at 2:30pm on August 4 2026.

## Analogy

Like setting a one-time alarm for a specific date instead of a recurring one.
