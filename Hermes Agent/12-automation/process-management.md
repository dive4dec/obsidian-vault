# Process Management

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

Hermes can track and manage background processes: list them, check status, and kill them if needed.

## Concrete Example

You list running processes, see one is stuck, and kill it to free up resources.

## Analogy

Like a task manager on your computer showing all running apps with the option to close them.
