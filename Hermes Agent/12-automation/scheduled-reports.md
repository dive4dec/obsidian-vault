# Scheduled Reports

> **Domain:** [[_automation-scheduling-moc|Automation & Scheduling]]

## Motivation

Cron jobs are great for generating and delivering reports on a schedule without anyone asking.

## Concrete Example

Every Friday at 5pm a cron job generates a weekly summary and sends it to your Telegram.

## Analogy

Like a weekly newsletter that arrives automatically every Friday without you having to request it.
