# Home Assistant

> **Domain:** [[_mcp-integrations-moc|MCP & Integrations]]

## Motivation

Smart home control via Hermes. Control lights, switches, sensors, and home automation.

## Concrete Example

/skill homeassistant. Hermes: Turn off the living room lights. Home Assistant: lights off. Hermes: Set thermostat to 22. Done. Control your home from chat.

## Analogy

A smart home dashboard - but instead of opening an app, you just tell Hermes what to do. Home Assistant integration makes Hermes your smart home remote.
