# Local Chromium

> **Domain:** [[_mcp-integrations-moc|MCP & Integrations]]

## Motivation

Using a local Chrome/Chromium browser for Hermes browser automation. No cloud service needed.

## Concrete Example

Local Chromium: Hermes opens Chrome on your machine, navigates, and interacts. No external service. Free. Uses your computer resources.

## Analogy

Owning a car vs taking a taxi - local Chromium is your own car. No fare, but you maintain it. Browserbase is a taxi - you pay per ride but no maintenance.
