# Spotify Integration

> **Domain:** [[_mcp-integrations-moc|MCP & Integrations]]

## Motivation

Spotify playback control via Hermes. Play, search, queue, manage playlists and devices.

## Concrete Example

/skill spotify. Hermes: Search for lo-fi study music. Results: 10 tracks. Hermes: Play track 3. Spotify starts playing. Control music from your chat.

## Analogy

A universal remote for your music - you can search, play, and manage Spotify from any chat platform. Hermes becomes your music remote.
