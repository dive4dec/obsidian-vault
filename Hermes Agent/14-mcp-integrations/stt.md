# STT

> **Domain:** [[_mcp-integrations-moc|MCP & Integrations]]

## Motivation

Speech-to-Text. Converts voice messages to text. Providers: local faster-whisper (free), Groq, OpenAI, Mistral.

## Concrete Example

STT: you send a voice message. faster-whisper (local, free) transcribes: Hello Hermes, what is the weather? Hermes processes the text. No typing needed.

## Analogy

A court reporter - they listen to people speak and type everything. STT does this for voice messages, converting speech to text.
