# Video Tool

> **Domain:** [[_mcp-integrations-moc|MCP & Integrations]]

## Motivation

Video analysis and generation via Hermes. Analyze video content or create new videos.

## Concrete Example

Hermes: Analyze this video. Video tool: extracts frames, identifies objects, describes action. Or: Generate a 5-second video of ocean waves. Video created.

## Analogy

A film editor and director combined - they can watch footage and describe it, or create new footage from a description. The video tool does both for Hermes.
