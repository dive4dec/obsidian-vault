# Vision Tool

> **Domain:** [[_mcp-integrations-moc|MCP & Integrations]]

## Motivation

Image analysis via Hermes. The model can see and understand images. Uses auxiliary vision model.

## Concrete Example

You send a photo of a plant. Hermes vision: This is a pothos plant. It needs indirect sunlight and weekly watering. Image understood and described.

## Analogy

A friend who is good at art - they can look at a painting and tell you about it. The vision tool lets Hermes look at images and describe them.
