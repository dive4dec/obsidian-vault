# Hermes Status

> **Domain:** [[_development-contributing-moc|Development & Contributing]]

## Motivation

A command: hermes status [--all]. Shows component status: CLI, gateway, cron, memory, plugins.

## Concrete Example

hermes status. Output: CLI: running. Gateway: stopped. Cron: active (3 jobs). Memory: enabled. Everything visible at a glance.

## Analogy

A car dashboard - you see fuel, speed, engine temperature, and warning lights. hermes status is the dashboard for Hermes components.
