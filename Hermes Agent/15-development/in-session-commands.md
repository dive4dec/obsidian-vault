# In-Session Commands

> **Domain:** [[_development-contributing-moc|Development & Contributing]]

## Motivation

Commands available during a chat session. Slash commands (/) and their handlers. Processed by process_command().

## Concrete Example

/session: list sessions. /model gpt-4: change model. /tools: manage tools. /skills: browse skills. All processed within the current session without restarting.

## Analogy

Quick buttons on a microwave - start, stop, add 30 seconds. You press them while cooking. In-session commands are quick buttons for your Hermes session.
