# MIT License

> **Domain:** [[_development-contributing-moc|Development & Contributing]]

## Motivation

A permissive open-source license. Allows free use, modification, and distribution with minimal restrictions.

## Concrete Example

MIT License: Copyright (c) Nous Research. Permission is hereby granted, free of charge... to use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute... Very permissive.

## Analogy

A blank check with no conditions - you can use the money however you want. The MIT license lets you use Hermes however you want, with almost no conditions.
