# Open Source

> **Domain:** [[_development-contributing-moc|Development & Contributing]]

## Motivation

Hermes is open source under the MIT license. Anyone can use, modify, and distribute it freely.

## Concrete Example

MIT License: you can use Hermes for free, modify it, distribute it, even sell products built on it. No restrictions. Just include the license. Truly open.

## Analogy

A community garden - anyone can plant, harvest, and enjoy. No admission fee. Everyone contributes and benefits. Open source is a community garden for software.
