# Safety Net

> **Domain:** [[_development-contributing-moc|Development & Contributing]]

## Motivation

Features that prevent irreversible mistakes: checkpoints, approvals, /undo, session branching.

## Concrete Example

Safety net: /undo removes last exchange. /rollback restores files. Approvals prevent dangerous commands. /branch creates a copy to experiment. Multiple layers of protection.

## Analogy

A safety net under a trapeze artist - if they fall, the net catches them. Safety net features catch you before you make irreversible mistakes in Hermes.
