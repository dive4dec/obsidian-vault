# Troubleshooting

> **Domain:** [[_development-contributing-moc|Development & Contributing]]

## Motivation

Diagnosing and fixing Hermes issues. Common steps: hermes doctor, check logs, verify config, test tools.

## Concrete Example

Problem: Gateway not responding. Steps: 1) hermes doctor. 2) Check ~/.hermes/logs/gateway.log. 3) Verify config. 4) hermes gateway restart. Systematic diagnosis.

## Analogy

Car repair - the engine makes a noise. You check the oil, listen to the engine, test the battery. Step by step, you find the problem. Troubleshooting is car repair for Hermes.
