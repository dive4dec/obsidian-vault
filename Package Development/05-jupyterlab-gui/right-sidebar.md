# right-sidebar

> **Domain:** [[_jupyterlab-gui-moc|JupyterLab GUI]]

## 🎯 Motivation

The collapsible panel on the right of JupyterLab for property panels, inspector, and extension tabs. Toggled with `IRightSidebar`.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Click the inspector icon on the right to see contextual help; extensions register right-sidebar widgets too.

## 🔗 Analogy

The right sidebar is a reference shelf — secondary info sits on the right, visible when you need it.
