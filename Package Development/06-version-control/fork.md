# fork

> **Domain:** [[_version-control-moc|Version Control]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A `fork` is your personal copy of someone else's repository. It lets you freely experiment with or modify a project you do not own, then contribute back via a pull request.

## 📋 Concrete Example

You click "Fork" on `github` to copy an open-source library, fix a bug in your fork, and submit a pull request to the original repo so the maintainer can review your fix.

## 🔗 Analogy

Forking is like photocopying a book from the library. You can write notes all over your copy without damaging the original, and later suggest your improvements to the author.
