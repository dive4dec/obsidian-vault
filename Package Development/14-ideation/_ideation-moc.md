# Package Ideation MOC

> **Domain:** [[_ideation-moc|Package Ideation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Great packages start with clear thinking about what to build and why. Ideation skills — finding problems, defining scope, and planning growth — separate projects that thrive from those that stall.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Before coding `timeflex`, you write a one-page plan: the pain point, the minimal API, the scope boundary, and three existing alternatives with their gaps. This page guides every later decision.

## 🔗 Analogy

Chefs plan a menu before cooking: they pick dishes, check what ingredients exist, and decide what to make vs buy. Cooking without a plan leads to wasted food and confused diners.
