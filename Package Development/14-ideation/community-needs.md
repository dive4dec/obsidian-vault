# Community Needs

> **Domain:** [[_ideation-moc|Package Ideation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Listening to the community reveals problems you may not face yourself. Forums, issue trackers, and surveys uncover gaps where a package would save many people real time.

## 📋 Concrete Example

You see dozens of Stack Overflow questions asking how to convert `.docx` to Markdown. That pattern signals a community need worth packaging, even if you personally only needed it once.

## 🔗 Analogy

A shopkeeper watches what customers ask for but cannot find. If ten people a day request umbrellas, stocking umbrellas serves a real need — not just the owner's personal wish list.
