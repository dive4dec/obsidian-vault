# Gap Analysis

> **Domain:** [[_ideation-moc|Package Ideation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Before building, check what already exists and where it falls short. Gap analysis prevents duplicating effort and pinpoints the specific improvement your package will offer.

## 📋 Concrete Example

You find three timetable parsers, but none export to Google Calendar. That gap — "parse plus Google export" — is your opportunity. Your package fills a real void, not a crowded space.

## 🔗 Analogy

Before opening a café, you walk the street counting existing coffee shops. If three serve espresso but none serve tea, a tea-focused café fills a gap rather than fighting established rivals.
