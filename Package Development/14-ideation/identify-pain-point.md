# Identify Pain Point

> **Domain:** [[_ideation-moc|Package Ideation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

Before writing code, clearly articulate the problem you are solving. A sharp problem statement guides every design decision and helps you resist adding features that do not serve it.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Instead of "a better spreadsheet tool," write: "Converting a `.xlsx` timetable to `.ics` calendar takes 20 manual steps. My package does it in one command." The pain is specific and measurable.

## 🔗 Analogy

A doctor diagnoses the illness before prescribing medicine. If they just handed out random pills, most would be useless. Naming the pain precisely is the diagnosis step for your package.
