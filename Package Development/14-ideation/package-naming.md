# Package Naming

> **Domain:** [[_ideation-moc|Package Ideation]]

## 🎯 Motivation

A package name is forever — it appears in imports, docs, and URLs. A clear, unique, memorable name makes your package easy to find and pleasant to use.

## 📋 Concrete Example

Name your package `timeflex` — short, unique on PyPI, and hints at "flexible timetables." Avoid names like `util2` (too generic) or `my_cool_parser` (too personal and hard to remember).

## 🔗 Analogy

Naming a pet: you will say it thousands of times, so pick something short and distinct. "Buddy" is too common at the dog park; "Mochi" turns heads and is easy to call.
